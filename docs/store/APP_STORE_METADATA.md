### Description (1575 / 4000 characters)

```text
Midnight Syndicate Street is an arcade street racing game set in a city at night, where it rains. Climb an underground racing ladder through ten career events: sprints, circuit races and time trials, run in daylight, at sunset and on rainy nights.

The first six events take place in Arden Bay, a fictional port city. The last four are World Tour events on two fictional circuits inspired by Istanbul: Maslak, with its towers and viaduct, and Bogaz, a coast road with a suspension bridge.

Races are short and easy to read. Drift to fill your nitro, then spend it on a burst of speed when the road opens up. The controls are touch based.

Each race has rival drivers in their own cars, with different skill levels. On some events, ambient traffic shares the road.

Win races to earn cash and reputation. Spend cash in the garage on paint, finishes and rims, and on performance upgrades for the engine, tyres, nitro and weight. Every race ends on a results screen with stars: first place earns three, a podium finish earns two, and any finish earns at least one. Time trials give stars for beating set times.

The cars are original designs: Noctra Sable, Noctra Vela, Arden Bruiser, Kestrel GT and Bosfor Atmaca. The replay camera lets you watch a finished race again from several angles.

Also in the game:
- Touch controls, a pause menu with settings and restart
- Graphics presets: Low, Medium and High
- Turkish and English interface
- Progress saved on your device

The game runs fully offline. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it collects no data.
```
