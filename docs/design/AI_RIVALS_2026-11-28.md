# AI rivals, 2026-11-28 (package ai, branch wp/ai)

Status: measurement and diagnostic repair only. No AI behaviour change is committed. The one change tried
(slipstream in the follow cap) was measured, was mixed, and was reverted. Defence logic was not built.

## What was done
- `game/ai/diag_ai_stuck.gd`: the diagnostic failed to compile (`Identifier not found: Settings` at
  `city_builder.gd:225`). Cause: `var city: CityBuilder` and `var manager: RaceManager` are typed, so GDScript
  compiles `city_builder.gd` while the script is parsed, before the `Settings` autoload exists. Fix: those two
  variables are untyped (`Node`), the race state is a local constant (`STATE_FINISHED = 3`), and the RaceManager
  is created with `load(...).new()`. Verified: `-s res://ai/diag_ai_stuck.gd -- --cars=1` runs (solo lap 64.6 s, 0 stuck).
- `game/ai/diag_rivals.gd` (new): rival metrics on one race, city_01, Bogaz or Maslak. Car 0 is the scripted
  player line (an AIDriver at skill 1.0, passed to RaceManager as the player, which drives the rubber band).
  Other cars use skills 0.97 / 0.94 / 0.91 / 0.88 / 0.85. Seeds 1 to 4 set the noise phases.
  Usage: `godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --audio-driver Dummy --path game -s res://ai/diag_rivals.gd -- --track=bogaz --seed=1 [--set=draft_ms=0.75]`.
  Output: one line `RIVALS {json}`.

## Metric definitions (diag_rivals.gd)
- overtakes: a pair's race-distance order flips (hysteresis 2 m, pairs within 80 m). `overtakes_with_player`: flips involving car 0.
- follow %: share of car-ticks with another car 0 to 15 m ahead in our lane (2.5 m lateral).
- side-by-side: pair-seconds with overlap under 7 m along and 2.0 to 3.6 m lateral (summed over pairs, so it is not a time with any pair alongside).
- contacts: car-to-car collision starts. stuck: AIDriver recovery starts. teleports: moves over 5 m in one tick.
- finish spread: last minus first total time.

## Not done, and why
- Defence logic (one move, then hold): not built. Analysis: the existing `_clamp_lane` already refuses a lane move
  toward any car within 7 m along that is closer than car_width + side_clearance (2.7 m), and the attacker that matters
  sits inside the 2.5 m lane band. A defensive move toward it would be clamped to zero in almost every case, so it
  would be a no-op, not a defence. A real defence needs a change to the clamp rule, which is a rule about unfair
  blocking that the owner should decide first.
- Racing line (entry, apex, exit) and braking points per corner: the target profile and the racing line live in
  `world/track.gd` and the city builder, which are outside package ai. Inside game/ai only the lane bias
  (`bend_bias_*`) exists.
- Skill-based mistakes (late braking, wide exits at low skill) and the difficulty curve from events.gd: not built in this pass.
- Contact rate on Bogaz (up to 20 contacts in one race, seed 4): not diagnosed. Next step: `diag_rivals.gd --track=bogaz --seed=4`
  and a trace of the contact pairs with `diag_ai_stuck.gd --s=a,b` style windows.
- Owner decisions: none needed for the reverted experiment. A defence rule change (see above) changes what counts as unfair blocking.

## Package ai2: racing line, passing, defence (wp/ai2, 2026-11-28)

## Result
Nothing in this package beats the base on the diagnostic. The racing line, the commitment to passes and the
defence move were built and measured; all three made the race worse on contacts and side-by-side time. They are
kept behind switches that default to the base behaviour, so shipping defaults reproduce the base exactly.

## What is in the code
- `game/world/track.gd`: `get_line_offset(offset)` and `get_line_speed(offset)`. The racing line is the centre
  line pushed to the inside of each bend with the entry and exit pushed outside (LINE_GAIN 150 m per 1/m, look 25 m,
  margin 1.5 m inside the road edges, offsets clamped to `get_road_span`). Its speed profile comes from the curvature
  of the line itself. Built once in `_ready`; reversed routes flip the offsets and rebuild the profile (`_flip_line`).
- `game/ai/ai_driver.gd`:
  - `use_racing_line` (default false): steer to the line offset at the look-ahead point and use the line speed.
  - `pass_commit_s` (default 0): a started pass is kept for this long even if a side fills or a bend appears.
  - `defence_shift` (default 0 = off): one lateral move toward a faster car up to 8 m behind that is not yet
    alongside (closer than car_length is alongside), held until it passes, leaves sensing or `defence_hold_s` is up.
    Lane clamping (`_clamp_lane`, side_long_range 7 m) still stops the move into a car alongside.
  - Pass side: the fallback to the blocker's side was kept at base. Removing it (tried) made bogaz worse.
  - Difficulty: unchanged. Skill already scales the target speed (corner fraction). No braking-margin mapping was added.
- Tests: `game/tests/test_racing_line.gd` (line inside the road, apex offset on the inside of the sharpest bend,
  reversed track keeps the rules and restores exactly) and `game/tests/test_ai_defence.gd` (scripted defender with a
  faster attacker 7.5 m behind on the right; asserts the commanded shift toward the attacker).

## Reading
- The racing line makes every car take the same apex, so side-by-side time rises about five-fold and contacts rise.
- Committing to a pass (1.5 s) keeps cars alongside when they should back off.
- Defence: a commanded move was checked in a scripted test. The lane clamp holds the car while the attacker is inside
  7 m (alongside), so the physical move is almost always cancelled. A useful defence needs the clamp to allow a move
  away from the attacker, or a smaller alongside zone; neither was tried.
- Removing the blocker-side fallback in the pass choice also raised side-by-side time on bogaz (1.1 to 5.5 s).
