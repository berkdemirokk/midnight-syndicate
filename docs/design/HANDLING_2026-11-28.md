## What changed

1. `arcade_car.gd`: the car body gets a `PhysicsMaterial` with friction 0.05 and bounce 0.1 (constants `BODY_FRICTION`, `BODY_BOUNCE`). Body contacts only: walls, props, other cars. Tyres are rays, so grip, launch and drift are unchanged.
   - Glancing 15 deg wall at 120 km/h: Sable **0.63 -> 0.86**, Vela **0.56 -> 0.86**. Pure geometry gives 0.97 with zero bounce, so the rest is friction.
   - Head-on: 0.00 -> 0.05. It still loses almost all its speed, which is the intent.
2. `game/tests/test_handling_feel.gd` (new, 4 tests) pins: 0-100 band per class, 90 % top time < 25 s, yaw rise <= 0.25 s at 60/120 km/h and <= 0.35 s at 180 km/h, glancing hit >= 0.80 (Sable and Vela), head-on < 0.5, nitro gain 60-120 >= 0.5 s.

No spec values were changed, so each class keeps its grip, mass and power.

## Checks

- `test_handling_feel`: 4 tests, 0 failed (after).
- Full suite after the change: **471 tests, 0 failed** (467 baseline + 4 new), 5 min headless. `test_ai_race` is part of that run and passes, so the AI finishes its races with the new body material.

## Not done, and why

- No real renders, no GPU captures, no before/after sheet. This pass changes physics only, and the camera is untouched. The drive telemetry scenario (`devtools/scenarios/drive_telemetry*.gd`) was not run: the probe is headless and measures the same quantities.
- Speed sensation (camera FOV, shake, pull-back) was not retuned. It needs a real render and a look at the capture, which this pass did not make.
- 90 % top speed is 21.7 s on the Sable. The approach is long because the top gear is a drag equilibrium. Fixing it means retuning torque curve, gear ratios and drag together, and the top-speed test (286 +-4 %) would move with them. Left for a dedicated tuning pass.
- Drift and the steering feel already met the bands; no change was made there.
- `BODY_FRICTION` and `BODY_BOUNCE` are constants in `arcade_car.gd` because `car_spec.gd` is not in this package's owned files. Moving them into `CarSpec` is the clean follow-up (owner or lead decision).

## Needs a human playtest

- Wall and prop scrapes at 120-250 km/h: does the 0.05 friction feel like momentum kept, or like the car skating? Head-on stops are still near dead; check that it does not feel like a wall.
- Drift entry, hold and exit on a phone with touch steering (the probe uses the car input directly, not the touch zones).
- Nitro kick at 60-120 km/h felt against the FOV kick in `camera/chase_camera.gd`.
