## What changed

1. `arcade_car.gd`: the car body gets a `PhysicsMaterial` with `BODY_FRICTION = 0.05` and `BODY_BOUNCE = 0.1`. Body contacts only (walls, props, other cars). Tyres are rays, so grip, launch and drift are unchanged.
2. `test_handling_feel.gd`: 0-100 band per class; the 90 % top-speed time is now pinned per class to about +-8 % of the measured value (`TIME90_BAND`), replacing the loose "< 25 s" check; yaw rise bounds; glancing wall >= 0.80 (Sable and Vela); head-on < 0.5; nitro gain >= 0.5 s.

## Mechanism

The friction sweep is monotone (1.0 -> 0.5 -> 0.05 gives 0.63 -> 0.75 -> 0.86), so body friction is the lever. Which combine rule the engine applies between the body and the wall material (average or minimum) was **not** verified, so the effective coefficient is not stated here. The earlier claim that "pure geometry gives 0.97 with zero bounce" was not measured and is withdrawn.

## Not done, and why

- **Drift entry and hold, speed-sensitive steering, counter-steer assist, camera FOV, shake and pull-back: not retuned.** Most of this already exists in code, so the brief's "add" was really "tune": steering lock scales with speed (`steer_angle_low_deg`, `steer_angle_high_deg`, `steer_speed_exponent` in `arcade_car.gd` step 4); counter-steer assist while drifting (`counter_steer_start`, `counter_steer_assist`); drift entry on handbrake or slip; camera FOV rises with speed (`fov_rest` to `fov_top`, `camera/chase_camera.gd`), speed shake from 180 to 240 km/h, nitro FOV kick and punch. Tuning these needs before/after captures from the capture harness (GPU lock), which this pass did not make. Owner or lead decision on the next pass.
- **90 % top speed is slow** (Sable 21.7 s, GT 20.8 s, Atmaca 16.5 s): the top gear is a drag equilibrium. Fixing it means tuning torque curve, gear ratios and drag together, and the top-speed test (286 km/h +-4 %) moves with them. The pinned bands above lock the current values on purpose so a regression is caught; a tuning pass must move them deliberately.
- **No renders, no drive telemetry capture.** The probe is headless. A before/after scrape clip (capture harness, `--capture=<scenario>`) is required before the scrape change is called shipped, because it changes how a scrape feels. No scrape scenario exists yet.
- `BODY_FRICTION` and `BODY_BOUNCE` are constants in `arcade_car.gd` because `CarSpec` is not in this package's owned files. Moving them into `CarSpec` is the clean follow-up (lead decision).
- `PROGRESS.md` is not in this package's sparse set; the lead adds this pass's line there.

### Top-speed approach (brief item 1)
Cause: the engine drive is cut at `top_speed_kmh` (a hard cap), so top speed is the spec value and the time to 90 %
is set by the force between 0 and 257 km/h. Drag was the main loss at 200+ km/h, and the Sable's high-rpm torque
fell away above 0.75 of redline.

Changes (spec files and `game/vehicles/specs/**` only):
- Drag: Sable 0.42 to 0.20; Vela, Tayfun, Bruiser and Kestrel GT x0.30; Atmaca x0.45 (its 0-100 would leave the band otherwise).
- Sable torque curve: 0.75 to 1.10, 0.88 to 1.25, 1.00 to 1.10 of peak, with `max_value = 1.3` on that curve. Peak torque 520 Nm unchanged.
- Legacy engine force curve tail (Vela, Tayfun, Bruiser, GT, Atmaca): ratio 0.8 from 0.33 to 0.42, ratio 1.0 from 0.21 to 0.30.
- Gear ratios are unchanged (tested: no gain). Top speed is unchanged (286, 232, 268, 312, 268, 140 km/h).

### Speed-sensitive turn-in (brief item 3)
`arcade_car.gd`: turn-in rate = `steer_speed` x lerp(1.35 at rest, 0.80 at top speed, speed/top). Centring keeps
`steer_return_speed`. Constants, not spec fields (`car_spec.gd` is outside this package).

Yaw rise after a full-lock step (time to 90 % of steady yaw, 60 Hz, one tick = 0.017 s):

| Car | 60 km/h base / now | 120 km/h base / now | 180 km/h base / now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sable | 0.183 / 0.167 s | 0.100 / 0.100 s | 0.050 / 0.067 s |
| Kestrel GT | 0.233 / 0.217 s | 0.133 / 0.117 s | 0.133 / 0.133 s |
| Atmaca | 0.217 / 0.217 s | 0.233 / 0.250 s | 0.300 / 0.317 s |

Effect is small: one tick at most. The rise time is set more by tyre build-up than by the steer rate. A stronger
multiplier (1.6 / 0.55) gave the same Sable numbers and pushed Atmaca at 180 km/h to 0.40 s, so it was reverted.

### Camera (brief item 2)
Already in release: FOV from `fov_rest` 62 to `fov_top` 72, capped by `fov_cap_deg()` (MAX_HFOV_DEG 125), speed drop

value: `speed_shake_from_kmh` 180 to 150 (full at 240, unchanged). Pull-back at speed is not added.
