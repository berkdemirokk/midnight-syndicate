# Career economy rebalance (2026-11-28)

Owner brief: ROADMAP step 9. The first eleven events paid 27,300 cash at first place against 821,500 of cars and
upgrades. Goal: owning everything takes about 1.5 to 2 full career runs, a player who earns 2 stars on average can
buy each car before the events that need it, no event is unwinnable for lack of cash, no purchases exist.

## What changed (one table, four numbers)

- game/core/game_state.gd REWARD_CASH: 1000, 600, 300, 100 becomes 4000, 2400, 1200, 400 (1st to 4th).
- Unchanged on purpose: car prices (game/vehicles/car_catalog.gd), upgrade costs (UPGRADE_BASE_COST), the rep table,
  the starter car and its 5000 cash, every event id, order, track, opponent skill, level, multiplier and star rule.
- Existing saves keep their cash, cars and upgrades. Nothing is migrated.
- Consequence: the first race (ortakoy_night, multiplier 1.5) now pays 6000 at first place instead of 1500. That is
  the only visible change to the first race. If the owner wants its payout kept, that needs a per-event exception.

## Simulator

tools/economy_sim.py reads the real numbers from the GDScript sources (no copies). It plays one career at a 2-star
average (3/2/1 stars at 25/50/25 percent), in ladder order. A level needs a car at least as expensive as
LEVEL_NEED_PRICE (level 2: 4000, level 3: 15000, level 4: 28000; these are assumptions, not game code, which has no
car gate). When short it replays the best-paying event played so far. After the ladder it buys the rest.

Run: py -3.10 tools/economy_sim.py (sources) or py -3.10 tools/economy_sim.py --table 1000,600,300,100 (what-if).

| measure | before 1000/600/300/100 | after 4000/2400/1200/400 |
|---|---|---|
| full 3-star career cash (35 events at 1st or top tier) | 114,500 | 458,000 |
| cost of 10 cars plus full upgrades on 11 cars | 821,500 | 821,500 |
| cost / 3-star career | 7.17 | 1.79 (target 1.5 to 2.0) |
| 2-star average career cash | 59,150 | 236,600 |
| cost / 2-star career | 13.89 | 3.47 |
| races to own everything, 2-star player | 339 (9.7 careers of 35) | 93 (2.7 careers of 35) |
| replays while stuck, whole career | 304 | 58 |
| replays before the ladder's car gates | 42 | 7 |
| best-paying event (replay target) | istanbul_championship, 2750 a race | istanbul_championship, 11000 a race |
| level 2 car (atmaca 4000) | after 0 replays | after 0 replays |
| level 3 car (bruiser 15000) | after 16 replays | after 2 replays |
| level 4 car (meridian 28000) | after 26 replays | after 5 replays |

Note on the target: cash cost against a 3-star career is 1.79, inside the band. In race count a 2-star player needs
93 races, about 2.7 full careers, because a 2-star run earns about half the 3-star cash.

ROADMAP step 9 metric: first-place cash for the first eleven events was 27,300 at 1000 a unit; at 4000 it is 109,200.

## Not met (honest)

- The level 3 car is not affordable from the ladder alone. At a 2-star average the player reaches 15,000 after two
  replays of the best event so far (overpass_duel, race 6). Closing that needs an earlier level 3 event or a lower
  car price, both more numbers than the brief allows. No event is blocked: the replays always pay out.
- The need thresholds are the owner's call. Change LEVEL_NEED_PRICE in the sim to test others.
