# Audio package, 2026-11-28 (wp/audio): layered engine bands

Status: engine layer assets, the band helper and its tests are in. NOT yet wired into `CarAudio`,
not yet mixed or ducked, not listened to by anyone. Dev-PC checks only.

## What is in this package

- `tools/audio_synth/engine_layers.py` (numpy, stdlib WAV writer). Generates 32 seamless loops:
  4 engine types (`i4` inline-4, `v6` V6 with turbo, `v8` V8, `hy` 12-cylinder hyper) x 4 RPM bands
  x on-load / off-load. Output: `game/assets/audio/engine/<eng>_<band>_<load>.wav`, 16-bit mono 44.1 kHz,
  1.0 s, `smpl` loop chunk. Total 2.8 MB.
- Synthesis is in the frequency domain on an exact 1 s buffer (bin = 1 Hz). Firing fundamental
  `f0 = rpm/60 * cylinders/2`, so every harmonic and noise bin is periodic: the loop is seamless by
  construction. Band reference rpms (1500, 2700, 4500, 7200) are multiples of 300, so f0 is an exact integer
  for 4, 6, 8 and 12 cylinders. Harmonic slope is steeper on-load than off-load. Intake noise is a
  lorentzian band that grows with rpm. Exhaust resonance peaks (2 per engine) act on every line.
  Off-load: softer harmonics, uneven amplitudes (overrun crackle), 1.8x noise.
- `game/audio/engine_layers.gd` (`class_name EngineLayers`): `band_weights(rpm, throttle)` returns 8
  equal-power weights (band x load, sum of squares = 1); `band_pitch(rpm, band)` clamps the pitch to
  [1/1.3416, 1.3416]; `dominant_band(rpm)`.
- `game/tests/test_audio_layers.gd`: equal-power crossfade over an rpm/throttle sweep, dominant-band pitch
  inside the window, and all 32 loops registered in `AudioLib.FILES`, loading, flagged as loops.
- `game/audio/audio_lib.gd`: 32 `eng_<eng>_b<n>_<load>` entries added to `FILES`.
- `evidence/audio/spectrogram_<eng>.jpg`: 4 panels per engine (4 bands x on/off), STFT, 0 to ~7.7 kHz.

## Design choices with their limits

- Pitch window: band neighbours are 1.8x apart at most, so the edge between two bands needs a pitch
  ratio of sqrt(1.8) = 1.342 on each side. The window is therefore about -4.6 to +4.6 semitones. This is
  the chipmunk limit. A tighter window needs more bands (about 6 for 1000 to 7500 rpm) and the brief says 3 to 4.
  Owner decision: keep 4 bands (asset size, GPU-free work) or go to 6 bands with a tighter window.
- Loudness: all 32 loops share one global gain (RMS of the loudest is 0.16), so the relative level between
  bands and loads is kept from the synth. The final level belongs to the game (layer gains and bus levels).
- Idle (below 1120 rpm) and redline (above 9650 rpm) are outside the band window; `band_pitch` clamps there.

## Measured (synth output, dev PC, not a listening test)

- Harmonic-to-noise power ratio (sum over 200 to 5000 Hz, harmonics vs the rest):
  i4 band 1 on-load 13.4 dB, off-load 1.8 dB; i4 band 3 on-load 14.9 dB, off-load 3.3 dB;
  hy band 4 on-load 8.2 dB, off-load -3.5 dB (noise dominated, intentional for the hyper off-load crackle; not yet judged by ear).
- Peaks: 0.23 to 0.52 (no clipping; the synth asserts below 0.98).
- Test run: `test_audio_layers`: 3 tests, 0 failed; max equal-power error 1.1e-7; worst pitch ratio 1.342.
