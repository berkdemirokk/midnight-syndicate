# Audio package, 2026-11-28 (wp/audio): layered engine bands

Status: engine layer assets, the band helper and its tests are in. NOT yet wired into `CarAudio`,
not yet mixed or ducked, not listened to by anyone. Dev-PC checks only.

## What is in this package

- `tools/audio_synth/engine_layers.py` (numpy, stdlib WAV writer). Generates 48 seamless loops:
  4 engine types (`i4` inline-4, `v6` V6 with turbo, `v8` V8, `hy` 12-cylinder hyper) x 6 RPM bands
  x on-load / off-load. Output: `game/assets/audio/engine/<eng>_<band>_<load>.wav`, 16-bit mono 44.1 kHz,
  1.0 s, `smpl` loop chunk. Total 4.2 MB.
- Synthesis is in the frequency domain on an exact 1 s buffer (bin = 1 Hz). Firing fundamental
  `f0 = rpm/60 * cylinders/2`, so every harmonic and noise bin is periodic: the loop is seamless by
  construction. Band reference rpms (1500, 2100, 2880, 4020, 5520, 7200) are multiples of 60, so f0 is an exact integer Hz for 4, 6, 8 and 12 cylinders: the loop is periodic and plays exactly at its REF (the first 6-band set used 2900/4000/5500 and rounded f0, up to 6 cents off REF; fixed in lead review). Harmonic slope is steeper on-load than off-load. Intake noise is a
  lorentzian band that grows with rpm. Exhaust resonance peaks (2 per engine) act on every line.
  Off-load: softer harmonics, uneven amplitudes (overrun crackle), 1.8x noise.
- `game/audio/engine_layers.gd` (`class_name EngineLayers`): `band_weights(rpm, throttle)` returns 12
  equal-power weights (band x load, sum of squares = 1); `zone_lo` / `zone_hi` give each band's in-tune zone;
  `band_pitch(rpm, band)` clamps the pitch to [1/1.25, 1.25].
- `game/tests/test_audio_layers.gd`: equal-power crossfade over an rpm/throttle sweep; zone overlap structure;
  every audible loop in tune and no weight outside its zone; all 48 loops registered in `AudioLib.FILES`,
  loading, flagged as loops.
- `game/audio/audio_lib.gd`: 48 `eng_<eng>_b<n>_<load>` entries in `FILES`.
- `evidence/audio/spectrum_<eng>.jpg`: one sheet per engine, 6 bands x on/off, magnitude spectrum 0 to 1500 Hz
  (1 Hz bins, the loop is exactly 1 s so harmonic lines are exact), 60 dB range. Replaces the earlier
  spectrogram sheets, which were too compressed to show harmonics.

## Design: crossfade inside the in-tune zone

- Each band is in tune only inside its zone `[REF/1.25, REF*1.25]` (about +-3.9 semitones). A band's weight
  is zero outside its zone: it rises over its overlap with the band below and falls over its overlap with the
  band above, on a log-rpm equal-power curve. Zone overlaps exist because neighbour ratios are at most 1.4,
  below 1.25^2 = 1.5625. Bands two steps apart do not overlap, so at most two adjacent loops are audible.
- Earlier version (4 bands, window 1.342): weights crossed over the full gap between band centres, so a band
  kept weight while off by up to 3 semitones (about 9.5% of power at 2400 rpm). That is fixed.
- Limit: the played pitch always matches rpm, but a loop shifted by up to 3.9 semitones at its zone edge
  changes timbre (formants and exhaust peaks move). A tighter zone needs more bands: about 8 bands for +-2
  semitones over 1500 to 7200 rpm. Owner decision.
- Idle (below about 1200 rpm) and redline (above about 9000 rpm) are outside the outer zones; `band_pitch`
  clamps there (shortcut, documented in the code).
- Loudness: all 48 loops share one global gain (RMS of the loudest is 0.16). Measured RMS runs
  0.028 (i4 band 1 off-load) to 0.160, about 15 dB. Peaks 0.095 to 0.546, no clipping (the synth asserts below
  0.98). The game must set layer gains from these RMS values, not from peaks.

## Measured (dev PC, synth output and headless test, not a listening test)

- Equal-power error over the sweep: 1.4e-7.
- Audible loops per rpm and throttle: at most 2, adjacent. Every audible loop plays at rpm / REF with the clamp
  not engaged (0.0 cents off). Weight outside a zone: none (below 1e-6).
- Test run: full suite `471 tests, 0 failed` (exit 0), including the 4 `test_audio_layers` tests and
  `test_audio.gd::test_loops_flagged_and_seamless` on the new loops.
