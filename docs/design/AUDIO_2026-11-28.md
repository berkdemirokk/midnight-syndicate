# Audio package, 2026-11-28 (wp/audio): layered engine bands

Status: engine layer assets, the band helper and its tests are in, and the player engine is wired into
`CarAudio` (section "Wired" below). Not yet mixed against music or other buses, not listened to by anyone.
Dev-PC checks only.

## What is in this package

- `tools/audio_synth/engine_layers.py` (numpy, stdlib WAV writer). Generates 48 seamless loops:
  4 engine types (`i4` inline-4, `v6` V6 with turbo, `v8` V8, `hy` 12-cylinder hyper) x 6 RPM bands
  x on-load / off-load. Output: `game/assets/audio/engine/<eng>_<band>_<load>.wav`, 16-bit mono 44.1 kHz,
  1.0 s, `smpl` loop chunk. Total 4.2 MB.
- Synthesis is in the frequency domain on an exact 1 s buffer (bin = 1 Hz). Firing fundamental
  `f0 = rpm/60 * cylinders/2`, so every harmonic and noise bin is periodic: the loop is seamless by
  construction. Band reference rpms (1500, 2100, 2880, 4020, 5520, 7200) are multiples of 60, so f0 is an exact integer Hz for 4, 6, 8 and 12 cylinders: the loop is periodic and plays exactly at its REF (the first 6-band set used 2900/4000/5500 and rounded f0, up to 6 cents off REF; fixed in lead review). Harmonic slope is steeper on-load than off-load. Intake noise is a
  lorentzian band that grows with rpm. Exhaust resonance peaks (2 per engine) act on every line.
  Off-load: softer harmonics, uneven amplitudes (overrun crackle), 1.8x noise.
- `game/audio/engine_layers.gd` (`class_name EngineLayers`): `band_weights(rpm, throttle)` returns 12
  equal-power weights (band x load, sum of squares = 1); `zone_lo` / `zone_hi` give each band's in-tune zone;
  `band_pitch(rpm, band)` clamps the pitch to [1/1.25, 1.25].
- `game/tests/test_audio_layers.gd`: equal-power crossfade over an rpm/throttle sweep; zone overlap structure;
  every audible loop in tune and no weight outside its zone; all 48 loops registered in `AudioLib.FILES`,
  loading, flagged as loops.
- `game/audio/audio_lib.gd`: 48 `eng_<eng>_b<n>_<load>` entries in `FILES`.
- `evidence/audio/spectrum_<eng>.jpg`: one sheet per engine, 6 bands x on/off, magnitude spectrum 0 to 1500 Hz
  (1 Hz bins, the loop is exactly 1 s so harmonic lines are exact), 60 dB range. Replaces the earlier
  spectrogram sheets, which were too compressed to show harmonics.

## Design: crossfade inside the in-tune zone

- Each band is in tune only inside its zone `[REF/1.25, REF*1.25]` (about +-3.9 semitones). A band's weight
  is zero outside its zone: it rises over its overlap with the band below and falls over its overlap with the
  band above, on a log-rpm equal-power curve. Zone overlaps exist because neighbour ratios are at most 1.4,
  below 1.25^2 = 1.5625. Bands two steps apart do not overlap, so at most two adjacent loops are audible.
- Earlier version (4 bands, window 1.342): weights crossed over the full gap between band centres, so a band
  kept weight while off by up to 3 semitones (about 9.5% of power at 2400 rpm). That is fixed.
- Limit: the played pitch always matches rpm, but a loop shifted by up to 3.9 semitones at its zone edge
  changes timbre (formants and exhaust peaks move). A tighter zone needs more bands: about 8 bands for +-2
  semitones over 1500 to 7200 rpm. Owner decision.
- Idle (below about 1200 rpm) and redline (above about 9000 rpm) are outside the outer zones; `band_pitch`
  clamps there (shortcut, documented in the code).
- Loudness: all 48 loops share one global gain (RMS of the loudest is 0.16). Measured RMS runs
  0.028 (i4 band 1 off-load) to 0.160, about 15 dB. Peaks 0.095 to 0.546, no clipping (the synth asserts below
  0.98). The game must set layer gains from these RMS values, not from peaks.

## Wired into CarAudio (wp/audio2, 2026-11-28)

- Player (`layered_engine = true` and all 48 loops load): 4 voices (2 band slots x on/off load). Each frame
  `EngineLayers.band_weights(rpm_abs, throttle)` gives the weights; the two adjacent bands with weight above
  1e-4 hold a slot. A slot is freed when its band is silent and both voices have faded (below 0.001). A new band
  that finds both slots taken may take the quieter slot whose band is silent once its voices are below 0.1 (-20 dB):
  after an upshift the rpm falls fast through two or three bands, and without this both slots still held the fading
  bands of the old rpm, so the engine fell to about -60 dB for 150 ms after every upshift (lead review, visible as a
  dark column after each shift in the earlier spectrogram). The race trace test now checks the level never drops out. Voice gain is smoothed (about 25 ms), pitch is `band_pitch`.
- `rpm_abs = idle_rpm + rpm_norm * (redline_rpm - idle_rpm)` from the car spec (1000 / 7500 rpm defaults).
- Loudness: each loop is scaled to `EngineLayers.TARGET_RMS` (0.10) using the measured RMS in
  `EngineLayers.LOOP_RMS`. The level is a choice, not a listened one; LAYER_GAIN 0.8 (-2 dB) matches the old layers.
- Engine per car: `EngineLayers.engine_for_spec(spec)` by top speed (<= 200 i4, <= 270 v6, <= 320 v8, else hy),
  or a spec meta `engine_type` ("i4" | "v6" | "v8" | "hy") as an override. Shortcut: specs carry no engine field,
  so this is a guess from top speed. Current mapping (top speed from `game/vehicles/specs/*.tres`):
  i4 Velmar Strix 140, Velmar Strix S 170; v6 Noctra Vela 232, Arden Bruiser 268, Noctra Cyclon 268;
  v8 Noctra Sable R 286, Arden Havoc 300, Noctra Meridian 308, Kestrel GT 312, Kestrel Marten 316;
  hy Kestrel Vanta 330, Velmar Fulmen 352. No spec has an engine_type meta yet.
- Upshift cut: 60 ms throttle dip to 0.4 and volume duck to 0.3 (fast attack and release). The old 200 ms rpm dip and
  200 ms level dip (`_shift_gain` 0.55) both apply on the layered path, as they did on the old one.
- Limiter bounce: from rpm_norm 0.98 with throttle above 0.5, the engine gain bounces at 16 Hz with a 45% dip.
- Opponents: one loop, the old `engine_mid_loop`, at the mid pitch (no high layer). Its level follows rpm as the
  old path did (low+mid share of the layer weights), floored at 0.5 (-6 dB) because the mid weight is zero at
  redline and the old formula would have silenced the rival there. Player fallback when
  `layered_engine` is false or a loop is missing: the old 3 single loops, unchanged.
- Tests: `game/tests/test_audio_engine.gd` (voice cap 4 player / 1 rival, crossfade power, mapping per spec and
  override, fallback, upshift cut, limiter bounce, 20 s race trace). `test_audio.gd` keeps the old single-loop checks
  on the fallback path (its rig defaults to `layered_engine = false`).

Measured on the dev PC (RTX 5060 Ti, headless Dummy audio, script time only; no mixer or phone numbers):
- Max engine voices playing in the sweep test: player 4, rival 1. In the 20 s race trace: player max 4, two bands
  held for 11.25 s of 20 s, upshift cut 0.25 s total (5 cuts).
- `CarAudio.update_audio` per frame over the 20 s trace, two runs: average 18 to 21 us, max 97 us and one 998 us spike
  (noisy dev PC, one car). Shot and loop voices of the rest of the car are not counted here. Not a phone number.
- Rival level: mid voice -12 dB at 0.3 rpm, -18 dB at redline (floored; the old formula went to -90 dB there).
- Limiter bounce: min 0.57, max 0.99 of the engine gain.
- Full suite (headless, dev PC): 510 tests, 1 failed: `test_first_race.gd::test_a_newcomer_pace_finishes_first_or_second`
  (4 of 4). Run alone, the same test passes twice (2nd place, 55.2 s). See Open.

Evidence (`evidence/audio/`):
- `race20_log.csv`: 1200 frames, the test's race profile (3 s cycles, upshift per cycle, 0.4 s at the limiter, 0.2 s lift-off):
  gear, rpm, throttle in and effective, duck, bounce, held bands, per-voice gain and pitch, voice count, update time.
- `race20_player_mix.wav`: 20 s mono 22.05 kHz, offline mix of the same loops at the logged gains and pitches
  (`tools/audio_synth/render_race_mix.py`). Peak 0.226, RMS 0.073, no clipping.
- `race20_player_spectrogram.png`: 0 to 1500 Hz, 70 dB linear colour ramp (the earlier scale went white along every
  harmonic). Shows the harmonic sweeps, the drop at each upshift and the flat top at the limiter. The 0.2 s lift-off is
  in the CSV (71 frames) but is not visually distinct in the plot.
