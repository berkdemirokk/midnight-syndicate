# Rating strategy research, 2026-11-27

Question from the owner: "Asphalt 4.7 almış App Store'da, hedef onu geçmek" and "ultra gerçekçi plan".
Method: three independent planners (rating, visual, release) read the repository and public sources, then a
red-team critic checked their claims against the code and the stores. Store figures are iTunes lookups of
2026-11-27 and move daily. Nothing here was measured on an iPhone. The order of work that came out of it is in
ROADMAP.md; this file is the evidence behind it and is not updated.

## Red-team verdict

Out-producing Asphalt (licensed cars, the Ferrari render, content volume, a million ratings) is not winnable and should stop being the yardstick; showing a higher average than its 4.70 on the Turkish storefront is arithmetically possible, but only on tens of ratings, where two one-stars in thirty put the display back at 4.7, and comparable small racers hold 112 to 701 Turkish ratings after seven to nine years. My estimate for a first version that does these twelve steps is a fair, stable game at roughly 4.5 to 4.8 on a small and fragile count around February to March 2027, and even that depends on two things no plan controls: the owner returning real phone data within a day each round, and the owner bringing the first players himself.

## Ordered steps (critic)

### 1. Free facts and decisions from the owner before any work

- From: PLAN (release) step 2, item 0 (five answers, App File Sizes); PLAN (rating) step 2 owner line and step 8 price decision; PLAN (visual) step 8 download question
- Why this rank: It costs no lead time and re-ranks everything below. If the uploaded build did not launch or steer, step 2 changes. The phone model decides which device class has any evidence. Free against paid changes the economy, the listing and the value of the rating prompt.
- Acceptance: PROGRESS.md records in the owner's words: launched, could steer, finished a race, heat, stutter; iPhone model and iOS version; a screenshot of App Store Connect > TestFlight > build > App File Sizes. Written yes or no on: free with no ads or purchases for 1.0; whether an iPhone 11, 12, 13 or SE 3 can be borrowed; the dark-studio garage; downloading the licensed traffic and parked car models. BLOCKERS.md B1 and B2 rewritten to match (B1 is stale: iOS builds and one upload exist).
- Needs the owner: Yes: 10-15 minutes of answers and one read-only store-console screenshot. Download approval if he says yes to the traffic models. No money.

### 2. Touch survival build: pause, quit, reset, auto-pause, a layout that does not cover the gauges, Turkish first run, A12 floor

- From: PLAN (rating) step 1; PLAN (release) step 1 and step 6 (smoke test on the iOS-filtered pack, tests before upload)
- Why this rank: Highest rating gain per day. Verified in code: pause is Esc or pad Start only (game/core/input_setup.gd), the touch layer has five zones and no pause or reset (game/vehicles/touch/touch_controls.gd), no script handles app pause, and by the layout constants the BRAKE zone covers the NITRO gauge and most of the tachometer. Each one is a one-star for every touch player, and all of it is testable on the desktop.
- Acceptance: New headless tests pass: a screen touch on the pause rect pauses the tree; an app-paused notification during a race leaves it paused on return; a car on its roof or parked at a wall is back on the line within 3 s; no touch zone intersects the minimap, tachometer or nitro rects at 2556x1179 and 2868x1320. Capture 'touch_layout' with zones forced visible, looked at by the lead. A smoke script on a pack exported with the iOS exclude filters runs menu, garage and 20 s of each event with 0 ERROR lines (covers the Capture autoload that devtools/* excludes). The CI log prints Info.plist with the A12 capability, and the iOS job runs the tests first. Owner, thumbs only: pause, resume, restart, quit, reset at a wall; swipe home mid-race and return to a paused game; a phone set to Turkish starts in Turkish; one launch in airplane mode.
- Needs the owner: Yes: approve the tag push that uploads build 2 to TestFlight, then a 10-minute device check. Steps 3 and 4 ride in the same build.

### 3. Texture memory diet

- From: PLAN (release) step 3
- Why this rank: Half a day of import settings against the crash class the owner's phone cannot show. Verified: 247 of 358 texture imports are lossless, 617 MB as RGBA8 with mipmaps; 174 of them are the photo kits. Memory kills land on 4 GB phones, which is most of the Turkish audience this game wants.
- Acceptance: The audit prints 0 lossless textures under game/assets/world and game/assets/vehicles (interface, icon and splash exempt). A test fails on any new lossless or wider-than-2048 3D texture. Harness video memory for bench_night_gameplay falls by at least 200 MB from 763.8 MB (High, desktop figure). Side-by-side crops of facades, the tail-light atlas and night glow maps signed by the lead. All tests green. Pack size is reported, not gated.
- Needs the owner: Light: a 5-minute look for blocky facades, tail lights and sky inside the step 2 device check. No download, no money.

### 4. Performance card inside the build; first iPhone numbers on the owner's phone and on one 4 GB phone

- From: PLAN (rating) step 2 (visible Settings entry, autopilot bench, soak, pass line); PLAN (visual) step 1 (Low and Medium captures first); PLAN (release) step 2 (cold-launch timing), cut to one tap and one screenshot
- Why this rank: No iPhone number exists and none can be taken today: devtools/* is excluded from the iOS export and phone builds render at scale 1.0. Default preset, render scale, 60 or 30 fps, the device floor and which visual work is worth doing all wait on this. It ranks after steps 2-3 only because it ships in the same build.
- Acceptance: Desktop first: Low and Medium night re-measured on all three tracks; the 2026-11-26 rows (Medium 25 ms average and 60 ms p95, Low p95 15.7 ms, both slower than High) explained or gone. Phone: GRAPHICS_BENCHMARK.md gets its first rows naming iPhone model and iOS version, all read from one screenshot of the result card: per preset 60 s of autopilot on Bosphorus night with average fps, share of frames over 20 ms and over 40 ms, worst frame, video memory, pipeline compilations during the race; a 20-minute unattended soak with minute 2 against minute 20 and any memory warning. Owner adds cold-launch seconds (first and second launch), a heat note 1-5, battery percent before and after, and an ear note on the sound with headphones. One pass line: at most 3% of frames over 20 ms, minute-20 average at least 55 fps, no frame over 100 ms after the countdown on a second run, no memory warning; a miss drops one preset or locks 30 fps and the run repeats. A dated decision line: default preset, 3D scale, 60 or 30, device floor. The same card from one iPhone 11, 12, 13 or SE 3; if none is found, that class is written down as untested.
- Needs the owner: Yes: device test (about 10 minutes hands-on, 25 unattended), finding a second phone, and a Beta App Review submission if the second phone joins through an external invite (store action). No money.

### 5. Crash, save and boot shield

- From: PLAN (rating) step 3 without item (f), which is already shipped; PLAN (release) step 6 (memory-warning handler, save fuzz tests)
- Why this rank: A crash or a lost save cannot be answered with a reply, and with tens of ratings one of them costs a tenth of a star. Mostly proof on code that exists: the atomic save with .bak is in game/core/game_state.gd. About 1.5 lead days, desktop-testable.
- Acceptance: Tests pass: saves of version 1 and 2 load with cash, cars and stars intact; a truncated main file falls back to .bak; a stray .tmp is ignored; a 'race open' flag left set lowers the preset once and says so in one line; a memory warning drops the mirror and one preset for the session; 2,000 random menu activations never leave Router busy longer than 2 s. Owner: update from build N to N+1 through TestFlight keeps cash, cars and stars; force-quit mid-race gives the one-line notice once and an intact save.
- Needs the owner: Yes: 15 minutes for the update and force-quit checks, and approval of the device floor (store-facing; it can be relaxed later, never tightened).

### 6. Touch controls tuned by thumbs: brake assist, sensitivity, left-hand and tilt options, live sliders

- From: PLAN (rating) step 5; PLAN (release) step 5 (live sliders, so one owner session replaces several builds)
- Why this rank: Controls are the top gameplay complaint in the plans' review samples, the steering was tuned on a keyboard, and nobody has driven this game with thumbs. It comes after step 4 because frame rate changes how steering feels.
- Acceptance: test_brake_assist: an Ortakoy lap with full throttle and autopilot steering has 0 wall contacts with the assist on. Settings show sensitivity in 3 steps, left-handed swap and a tilt calibrate button. The owner sends one screenshot of the tuned slider values; they become defaults in a commit that names the phone. Owner's five touch laps on Ortakoy night: best lap within 15% of the autopilot lap in the same build and at most 1 wall hit per lap, both shown on the results card.
- Needs the owner: Yes: one 20-30 minute tuning session on the phone.

### 7. First ten minutes: two taps to a short night race a newcomer can win, three hints, one guided upgrade, a loading card

- From: PLAN (rating) step 6; PLAN (release) step 5 (five first-timers); PLAN (visual) step 8 (night and blue hour first)
- Why this rank: The rating prompt and word of mouth only pay if session one ends in a win. Today event 1 is two laps against rivals at skill 0.80-0.76 with no help text and a black fade (game/race/events.gd, game/core/router.gd). About 2 lead days of data and interface work.
- Acceptance: test_first_run: a fresh profile reaches the countdown in 2 interface activations; event 1 is 1 lap with rival skills at or below 0.74 and no traffic; a bot at skill 0.72 with brake assist finishes first or second in 9 of 10 seeds. The ui_event_select capture shows the first three events as night or sunset, and a capture shows the loading card. On the phone: cold launch to driving in 30 s or less by stopwatch. Five people who never played: at least 4 finish first or second in race 1 without help and all find pause; where each got stuck is written to docs/beta/FIRST_RUN.md.
- Needs the owner: Yes: the stopwatch check and the five-person test, about 1 hour.

### 8. Rating request by Apple's rules, plus Rate and Support rows in Settings

- From: PLAN (rating) step 4; PLAN (release) step 9
- Why this rank: By the rating plan's own evidence the displayed averages come from the system star prompt (Apple allows it three times in 365 days and says to ask after a completed task, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/ratings-and-reviews/). Without it the game is rated mostly by people who write reviews. It ranks here because it pays only once steps 2-7 make a session worth rating. Start the native-build tries in week one in the background: it is the least testable item in all three plans.
- Acceptance: test_review_gate passes at least 12 cases (loss: no; 4 races: no; first session: no; second ask on the same version: no; win in session two after 5 races: yes, once). The iOS build with the plugin links, launches and survives the call on the phone, shown as a line on the performance card ('review gate: requested' with the time), because the owner cannot read device logs. The Settings rows 'Rate the game' (https://apps.apple.com/app/id<ID>?action=write-review) and 'Support' open on the phone. The store build has no question about liking the game and no reward. After release the owner sees the system sheet once.
- Needs the owner: Yes: download approval for the MIT plugin or the choice of a project-made one, the support contact and the App Store ID; confirmation after release.

### 9. A fair ladder: about 30 events from the three tracks, economy rebalance, Easy / Normal / Hard

- From: PLAN (rating) step 7
- Why this rank: Verified grind wall: 11 events pay 27,300 cash for first wins against 260,500 of cars and 51,000 of upgrades per car, and the top car is 18 wins of one race. In a free game that reads as 'boring after an hour'. It is data and tests only, 2-3 lead days, no art and no device, and more wins give the rating gate more good moments. It follows step 7 because it builds on the tuned rival skills.
- Acceptance: test_events: at least 30 events, each with Turkish and English names, and no event needs replays of earlier events to afford the cheapest eligible car. A one-off bot run, outside the default suite: a skill-0.85 bot earns 2 stars or more on every event within 3 attempts, replays already-won events at most 10 times, and owns every car within 90 races; the cash curve is written under evidence/. The results screen shows the next car with 'you have X of Y'. The owner plays the first 12 events on the phone and marks any event lost three times.
- Needs the owner: Yes for the 30-40 minute play-through note. No for the build.

Garage and title recaptured (overview, front, rear34, 2556x1179, and ui_main_menu with a car): 150 draws and 220,000 primitives or fewer on High, video memory below today's 580-639 MB. The day benchmark shot shows cast shadows and no project-made car within 300 m of the grid. A before/after sheet against the old garage, not against the Ferrari card, approved by the owner. Tests green.

### 11. Store pack: name check, price, privacy page, age rating, Turkish proofread, screenshots at an accepted size

- From: PLAN (release) step 7; PLAN (rating) step 8; PLAN (visual) step 10 (harness captures; the hero render is optional and only for icon, splash and posts)
- Why this rank: Mostly owner administration that blocks the public build, with about one lead day. A rejection costs days, and the name and device floor are hard to repair after launch. It comes late because the screenshots must show the final look and the real counts.

Six to eight harness captures at 2868x1320, night scenes first, touch pads hidden, each with its JSON. A test confirms the credits list every CC BY author in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md. A file records the lead's look-alike check of the eleven cars and of every generated sign and facade texture at 100%. App Store Connect shows no missing-field warning.

### 12. External TestFlight beta, release in Turkey first, then reply and fix every week

- From: PLAN (rating) steps 9 and 10; PLAN (release) steps 8 and 10, with exit rules that can be measured
- Why this rank: Last because it needs everything above. TestFlight feedback never touches the public rating, and a small first storefront keeps a bad week local. The plans' exit numbers need analytics and a tester pool that do not exist, so they are replaced by what App Store Connect and screenshots show. Bringing players is the owner's job and the weakest link of the whole goal.
- Acceptance: Beta exit: at least 10 testers, at least 2 of them on 4 GB phones; App Store Connect shows 0 crashes for the candidate build over at least 50 sessions; each tester sends one screenshot of the performance card and one of their event list; docs/beta/FEEDBACK.md has every item closed or answered. Release in Turkey with manual release. After launch: every review of three stars or less has a posted reply within 48 hours; a crash or 'stuck' review leads to a fix build within 7 days; evidence/ratings_log.csv gets a weekly row with this game's and Asphalt's Turkish averages. Other territories open after two crash-free weeks, not at a rating count.
- Needs the owner: Yes: every store action (Beta App Review, public link, release, territories), tester invitations and posts (messages), posting the review replies. No money.

## Unrealistic items the critic removed or changed

- Rating-count targets: 'Turkish average above Asphalt with at least 100 ratings, then the same in the US' (rating step 10) and 'widen at 30 Turkish ratings' (rating step 9, release step 10)
  Why: Turkish-storefront counts read today: Rush Rally 3 has 112 ratings after 7.7 years, DATA WING 701 after 9 years, GRID Autosport 260, GRID Custom Edition 54 (https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1206723870,1437096541,1179421849,1551356749&country=tr). No plan has a step that brings players, so 30 ratings can take many months and the 30-rating gate would hold the game in one storefront. At 30 ratings the display beats Asphalt's 4.70 from 148,620 (https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=asphalt+legends&country=tr&entity=software) only with at most one one-star: 28 fives and 2 ones give 4.73, shown as 4.7.
- Release plan device lab: 90-minute, 11-line owner protocol, three rounds, gates for three device classes, and 'at least 3 phones per class with lab JSON' as beta exit (release steps 2, 4, 8)
  Why: The owner's only device report so far is one word ('Denedim', PROGRESS.md session 7). There is no tester pool, and casual testers will not export JSON through the Files app. The owner's phone (1179x2556 screenshot) covers one class. What can really happen: one tap, an unattended run, one screenshot, plus one borrowed 4 GB phone.
- Rating step 4: '1-2 lead days' for the native rating prompt, accepted by 'the log line appears once on the phone'
  Why: The candidate plugin's page states no Godot version and shows no release information (https://github.com/godot-mobile-plugins/godot-inapp-review). A project-made plugin can only be compiled in the cloud Mac job (30-40 minutes per try, no local build). The owner has no Mac, so nobody can read a device log line: the acceptance cannot be run as written. Proof that the sheet appears comes only after public release. Budget a week of background CI tries and show the gate result on screen.
- Visual steps 4 to 7 (about 7 lead days: street light through sky radiance, puddle break-up, surface pass on eleven cars, facade depth)
  Why: All of it is judged on desktop High captures while nobody knows which preset an iPhone holds. The plan's own thesis says looks do not decide the rating. Step 4 rewires every reflective surface (probes, day ambient) for a saving that is an estimate from 3 of 11 cars. Step 6's grilles are not visible from the chase camera by the plan's own note. Steps 5-7 add draws and objects to a frame at 443,576 of 450,000 primitives. Park them until phone numbers exist.
- Rating plan date: Turkish launch in January 2027 after 12-14 lead days plus six short phone sessions
  Why: Every phone session is an owner round-trip plus a 30-40 minute CI build, and the first round-trip returned no data. Add Beta App Review, App Review, the name check and 2-3 weeks of beta. February to March 2027 is the honest window, and PROGRESS.md records that uploads need the iOS 27 SDK from April 2027, so the toolchain has a deadline too.
- Beta exit numbers: '80% of testers reached race 5', '100 sessions', 'median five-star answer 4 of 5' (rating step 9, release step 8)
  Why: The game is offline with no analytics and the store label is 'Data Not Collected'. TestFlight reports sessions and crashes, not progress. The release plan's question card needs each tester to send a file by hand. These numbers cannot be measured as written.
- Release step 3 acceptance: 'exported pack 200 MB or less' through VRAM compression
  Why: The memory finding is real: 247 of 358 texture imports are lossless, 617 MB as RGBA8 with mipmaps (my header audit: (session scratchpad, not kept) redteam/texmem.py). But compression cuts GPU memory, not download size: the same set is 77-154 MB as ETC2/ASTC against about 126 MB lossless in the pack today. The Windows pack (396,783,648 bytes, S3TC variants) is not the iOS size either. Gate on memory, report size.
- Three different pass lines for one phone measurement (visual step 1: minimum fps 55 over 5 minutes; rating step 2: at most 3% of frames over 20 ms; release step 4: p95 18 ms, battery 10% per 20 minutes, cold launch 5 s, per device class)
  Why: None of these comes from a device. A 'minimum fps' line fails on a single hitch. Three lines for the same run guarantee an argument instead of a decision. Pick one line (the rating plan's is the most usable) and let the first device run correct it.
- Visual step 10 hero render as the answer to the Ferrari card, and visual step 2 sign-off on a side-by-side with that card
  Why: A Cycles render of an 88k-triangle CC BY model is better lit, not licensed-car key art. It cannot be a store screenshot ('Screenshots should show the app in use', guideline 2.3.3, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) and the game has no in-app events to make an event card from. The side-by-side invites the one comparison this project cannot win.
- Stale numbers used as premises: 'Neon Wharf night 481 draw calls' as the heaviest scene (rating step 2, visual finding 4) and visual step 4's bar '470 draws or fewer (481 today)'
  Why: Today's capture shows Neon Wharf night at 423 draws / 410,342 primitives (evidence/screens/tod_tracks/city_night.json, 18:58). The visual bar is already met before any work. The heaviest frame by primitives is Bosphorus night (443,576).

## Contradictions between the plans and the project documents

- Redo of finished work (CLAUDE.md: 'Do not redo finished work'). Rating step 3(f), one shared tail-light atlas, already shipped in session 8 (PROGRESS.md, commit cfa70d7). Release step 6 says every colour-slider move writes the save; game/ui/garage/garage.gd:156 preview_paint does not save, commit_paint saves once.
- Screenshot size. Rating step 8 and visual step 10 plan 2556x1179 sets only. Apple requires a large-display set for an iPhone app: 2868x1320, 2796x1290 or 2736x1260, or the 6.5-inch sizes (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications). Release step 7 has it right. RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md step 26 is outdated too.
- Hidden against visible test screen. Release step 2 hides the Device Test screen behind taps on the version label. Rating step 2 makes it a visible Settings entry. Apple: 'Don't include any hidden, dormant, or undocumented features in your app' (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/). Use the visible entry, or keep the screen out of store builds.
- Rating solicitation. Rating step 4 promises no 'do you like the game' gate. Rating step 9 then asks only 'testers who liked it' to rate, and release step 8 adds an in-game 'would you give it five stars' card. Apple disallows custom review prompts (5.6.1) and names 'paid, incentivized, filtered, or fake feedback' as manipulation (same URL). The card must not exist in a store build; ask every tester or none.
- Performance direction. Visual step 4 adds a full-screen FXAA or SMAA pass on Medium and Low, and visual steps 5-7 add draws and objects. Release step 4 removes screen-reading passes below High and wants fewer objects, for heat. Release step 4 also pre-sets phone defaults (Medium, scale 0.75, dynamic resolution on) while game/core/graphics_preset.gd keeps scaling off until a device measurement confirms it.
- The reference card. The rating and release plans say the Ferrari card is unreachable and should stop being the yardstick. Visual step 2's acceptance is an owner sign-off on a sheet with that card on the left.
- Price and product identity. Rating plan: free, no ads, no purchases, zero revenue. GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT.md section 9: one premium price (first-pass $6.99). CLAUDE.md: 'commercially shippable'. Nothing is decided, and the decision blocks listing text, economy targets and the privacy label. It is the owner's call.
- Fiction rules against shipped content and listing. GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT.md section 2 and COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md section 5 forbid real cities and landmarks. The game ships the Ortakoy mosque, the Bosphorus bridge, a Turkish flag and Turkish plates; the rating plan sells 'Istanbul tracks' as the home advantage; docs/store/APP_STORE_METADATA.md says 'fictional Istanbul-inspired circuits', ten events and five old car names. The design document still promises police pursuit, 30 missions, drag and sprint. One source of truth is needed before any store text.
- Device floor against the evidence rule. Both release-minded plans set the floor at A12 and plan Low or 30 fps for A12-A13 phones, but nobody can test that class. Apple lets updates only keep or relax capability requirements (https://developer.apple.com/support/required-device-capabilities), so the safe order is strict first, relax after a tested phone exists.
- Turkey-only launch against the count the goal needs. One storefront with no marketing gives the smallest possible number of ratings, while a displayed average needs 'enough' ratings to exist (Apple publishes no number, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/ratings-and-reviews/). Widen by time and crash record, not by a rating count.
- Two rating triggers. Rating step 4: win or three stars, 5 finished races, second session. Release step 9: third podium, 20 minutes of play. Pick one.
- The same Windows pack is quoted as 397 MB (rating) and 378 MB (release). The file is 396,783,648 bytes (build/windows/midnight_syndicate.pck), built before session 8 removed 236 texture files. Neither figure is the iOS size.

## Missing from all three plans

- Players. No plan has a step that brings an audience, and the goal is a displayed average, which needs ratings to exist. The only free channel is the owner himself: short real-gameplay videos on his own accounts and Turkish App Store name, subtitle and keywords. Publishing and messages need his yes.
- A 4 GB iPhone. The owner's phone is a recent 6.1-inch model and will not show the memory kills that an iPhone 11, 12, 13 or SE 3 can get. The harness reports 764 MB of video memory on High and about 490-510 MB on Low (desktop figures, tod_tracks/*_low.json), so presets do not save memory. One borrowed older phone is worth more than three rounds on the owner's phone.
- Sound. BLOCKERS.md B5: 25 synthesised sounds, 'subjective sound quality not yet judged by ear'. The lead cannot hear. The electric Kestrel GT keeps an engine sound. Behaviour with the silent switch and with the player's own music is untested. A five-minute owner ear test with headphones belongs in the first device session, before any tester hears the game.
- First launch on Metal. Godot 4.7 docs: the Shader Baker shortens first-time startup on Metal but does not work with --headless exports (https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/performance/pipeline_compilations.html). .github/workflows/build-ios.yml exports with --headless and the iOS preset has no baker setting. Nobody has timed the first launch. A Godot forum thread reports an App Review rejection 'unresponsive on launch' (cause unconfirmed, https://forum.godotengine.org/t/ios-app-rejected-unresponsive-on-launch/130784). Time the cold launch first; if slow, try a non-headless export on the cloud Mac (unverified that the runner allows it) and show a loading screen before heavy loads.
- iPad run before App Review. The preset is iPhone-only, and Apple says 'iPhone apps should run on iPad whenever possible' (guideline 2.4.1). Only the release plan mentions it, as 'if one is at hand'. If no iPad exists, record it as an open review risk.
- Crash diagnosis without a Mac. The plans count crashes in App Store Connect, but nobody here can open a crash log in Xcode. Add a breadcrumb (last scene, preset, memory warning, unclean exit) shown on the performance card, so a tester's screenshot explains a crash. Whether App Store Connect serves readable logs on the web was not verified.
- Native Turkish proofread. The home advantage is the Turkish interface, every string was written by the AI lead, and the design document lists 'Turkish quality needs a native reviewer'. The owner reads all strings (about 200, game/ui/i18n.gd) once: 15 minutes.
- Intellectual property beyond the licence. CC BY covers the author's copyright ('Original work', 'No copied brands', https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/), not third-party design rights, and all eleven cars come from that one site. Generated facade and sign pictures can carry brand-like text. Needed before the public page: a recorded look-alike check of the eleven cars and of every sign texture, and the name check (the listing draft uses 'Midnight Syndicate Street'; an American music act uses 'Midnight Syndicate'; trademark status not verified here).
- A stop rule for bad phone numbers. Low still submits about 14,000 objects on the desktop, so a CPU-bound or memory-bound phone is not saved by any preset. No plan says what gets cut then (rival count, mirror, dressing density, a track) or when polishing stops and cutting starts.
- Round-trip economy. Every phone fact costs an owner session plus a 30-40 minute CI build, and no plan budgets builds. Rule: one build carries the touch fixes, the texture diet and the performance card; the next carries controls and first-run work; nothing goes to the owner for a single change.
- 'Works offline' is still a design rule only (RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md, claims not allowed yet), while the rating plan prints 'Offline' on the screenshots. An airplane-mode launch on the phone takes ten seconds and is in no plan.
- Not checked in this red-team pass: nothing was run in Godot or on a device; I looked at eight images; Apple's StoreKit page did not render, so the claim that the rating sheet stays hidden in TestFlight is not re-verified; the minimum rating count for a displayed average was not found; store figures are iTunes lookups from 2026-11-27 and move daily.

## Plan: rating lens

"Beat Asphalt" has two meanings. Out-produce it (licensed Ferraris, hundreds of cars, a studio): not winnable. The Ferrari store card is a marketing render of a licensed car; this project may not use real makes and cannot make that picture. Out-rate it: winnable, at small volume. The figure on the owner's Turkish storefront is 4.70 from 148,620 ratings; the US figure is 4.77 from about 1.07 million [S1][S2]. That figure is not what written reviews say: Asphalt's latest 100 US written reviews average 4.14, and NFS No Limits shows 4.75 while its latest 50 written reviews average 3.20 [S3][S5][S6]. My inference from those numbers: the average is carried by star-only ratings from the system prompt, shown to players at a good moment. Apple documents that prompt and tells developers to time it after a success [S15][S18]. Small fair games hold higher averages: DATA WING (free, no ads, no purchases, one developer) 4.88 from 23,959 ratings; Rush Rally 3 ($4.99) 4.78 from 3,049 [S8][S10]. What drags the big racers down in their own reviews: ads (8 mentions) and pay-to-win (5) among 21 low-star Asphalt reviews, pay-to-win (5) and ads (5) among 25 for NFS No Limits, then "the update made it worse", controls, crashes and a 3.95 GB download [S1][S5][S6]. What drags fair and premium racers down: crashes, lost saves, controls, unsupported devices, price [S9][S11]. Graphics are named in 3 of 34 five-star Asphalt reviews in the sample. So this plan does not chase the Ferrari picture. It removes every cause of a one-star, makes the first ten minutes a win, then asks for the rating the way Apple allows.

PRODUCT DECISIONS. Price: free, no ads, no purchases, no account in 1.0 (owner decides). Free-with-paywall editions of the same racer rate below the paid ones (Wreckfest 3.51 vs 4.24, GRID 4.35 vs 4.56) [S11][S12]. Revenue in 1.0 is zero: that is the honest cost. First ten minutes: two taps to a one-lap night race at Ortakoy that a newcomer wins or finishes second, one guided upgrade, three races done by minute ten, Turkish chosen automatically, nothing asked of the player. Session: races 60-120 s, never above about 3.5 min; three races plus a garage change in under ten minutes; no timers. Difficulty: any finish opens the next event (keep this rule), Easy/Normal/Hard offered after two bad finishes, no event may require replaying old events for cash. Controls: auto-throttle plus a new brake assist on by default, tap zones tuned on the phone, sensitivity / left-hand / tilt options, pause and reset always on screen. Rating: system prompt only, second session or later, five races done, right after a win, once per version; a Rate link and a Support link in Settings. Never: crash, lost save, stuck car, no way to pause or quit, uncapped heat, a store page that promises what is not in the game.

STATE TODAY (code read 2026-11-27). On a phone there is no pause, quit or reset path (keyboard and gamepad only) and no pause on interruption. Every preset renders at full native resolution on phone builds. The frame-time probe is excluded from the iOS build. There is no first-run help. 11 events pay 27,300 cash for a first win each, against 821,500 of cars and upgrades. Steps 1-8 are about 12-14 lead days plus six short phone sessions from the owner, then two to three weeks of beta. A Turkish launch in January 2027 is realistic only if the phone numbers from step 2 are acceptable.

### Winnable

- A displayed average above Asphalt's on the storefront where the game launches: 4.70 to beat in Turkey, 4.77 in the US [S1][S2]. Arithmetic: with only fives and ones, 4.8 allows one one-star per nineteen five-stars.
- Zero ads, zero pay-to-win, zero energy or timers: the top complaint classes in the Asphalt and NFS No Limits review samples [S5][S6]. "Free, no ads, no purchases" is itself a praise theme in about 15 of 47 five-star DATA WING reviews [S10].
- Download size: Asphalt is 3.95 GB and NFS No Limits 3.84 GB [S1][S3]; this game's pack is 397 MB on Windows (iOS size not measured yet).
- Offline, no login, no account to lose, two taps to a race.
- Home ground on the Turkish storefront: Turkish interface and Istanbul tracks. NFS No Limits lists no Turkish among its languages; Asphalt does [S14].
- Stability on a narrow, declared device range (A12 and newer) instead of promising every iPhone.
- Touch controls a first-timer can use, with assists and options. Controls and controller support are the top praise theme in the Rush Rally 3 sample (about 9 of 33 five-star reviews) [S9].
- The night-rain look at phone size. The real night captures (wet road, neon, Ortakoy bridge) are the strong part of the game; the day capture and the studio car are not.
- A finite, fair progression: every car earnable in hours of new events, not by repeating one race.
- A personal reply to every low review. Apple notifies the reviewer and lets them change the rating [S18]. A studio with a million ratings cannot do this; one owner with fifty can.

### Not winnable

- The Ferrari key-art level: licensed real cars and offline-render fidelity. Hard rule (no real makes), CC BY low-poly models and the Mobile renderer rule it out. Stop measuring the game against that card.
- Content volume: hundreds of cars, live seasons, multiplayer, events every week.
- Rating volume, chart rank and social proof: about 1.07 million US ratings against tens or hundreds here with no marketing budget. Paid small racers gathered 144 to 3,049 US ratings over years [S8][S11][S13].
- Daytime realism: no GI, SSAO or SSR on the Mobile renderer, and Forward+ broke the scene (BLOCKERS B12). The day capture shows it. Choose night.
- Device coverage and QA: one test iPhone against a studio's device lab. Mitigated by a device floor and self-healing, not solved.
- Revenue in 1.0 if the game is free. The rating goal and the money goal conflict in the first version.
- Proof before launch: Apple's rating prompt does nothing in TestFlight builds [S16], and iPhone numbers come only from the owner's phone.

### Steps

#### 1. Make the touch build safe: pause, quit, reset, auto-pause

- Why: On an iPhone the player cannot pause, restart, open settings or leave a race, and cannot free a stuck or flipped car. Pause is bound to Esc / pad Start and reset to R / pad Back only (game/core/input_setup.gd, game/ui/menus/pause_menu.gd); the touch layer has steer, brake, nitro and drift zones and nothing else (game/vehicles/touch/touch_controls.gd). No script handles app interruption. The Haptics toggle does nothing (no vibrate call in the game). Each of these is a one-star by itself and hits every touch player.
- How: (a) Pause button in the top safe corner of the HUD that calls pause_menu.pause(). (b) race_scene pauses on NOTIFICATION_APPLICATION_PAUSED and FOCUS_OUT while racing and stays paused on return [S23]. (c) Reset button in the touch layer, plus auto-reset through the existing reset_player() when the car is on its roof, or stopped off the line, or going the wrong way for 3 s. (d) Wire Settings.haptics to Input.vibrate_handheld for nitro, hits and countdown [S24], or remove the toggle. (e) First launch selects Turkish when the device language is Turkish (default is English today).
- Acceptance: New headless file game/tests/test_touch_survival.gd passes: a simulated screen touch on the pause rect pauses the tree; NOTIFICATION_APPLICATION_PAUSED during a race pauses it; a car put on its roof or parked against a wall is back on the line within 3 s; no vibrate call when haptics is off. A harness capture 'touch_layout' at 2556x1179 with the touch zones forced visible shows pause and reset clear of the other zones (no capture of the touch layout exists today). Owner on the phone: start a race, swipe to the home screen, return: the pause menu is up; Quit to menu works; stop sideways against a wall: the car resets.
- Effort: 0.5-1 lead day, 10 minutes of owner time
- Needs the owner: Yes: start the TestFlight build and do the three phone checks.

#### 2. Measure the game on the real iPhone (benchmark screen inside the build)

- Why: No iPhone frame time, heat or memory number exists. Heat, lag and crashes are review killers (CarX Street heat complaints in COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md; crashes and bugs lead the low-star GRID sample [S11]). Two code facts make guessing dangerous: phone builds render at 100% native resolution on every preset and dynamic resolution stays off until a device measurement sets scaling_confirmed_on_device (game/core/graphics_preset.gd); and the iOS export excludes devtools/*, so devtools/perf_probe.gd is not in the build (game/export_presets.cfg). Today nothing can come off the phone as a number.
- How: Move a small frame-time recorder into shipped code. Add a visible Settings entry 'Performance test' (visible and named in the review notes, not a hidden feature). The existing autopilot drives the heaviest scene (Neon Wharf night, 481 draw calls on the dev PC) for 60 s on Low, Medium and High, at full and reduced render scale, then an optional 20-minute soak on the chosen preset. The result card shows device model, iOS version, average fps, share of frames over 20 ms and over 40 ms, worst frame after the countdown, pipeline compilations during the race [S26], engine and video memory, minute 2 against minute 20, and any iOS memory warning. The owner screenshots the card; nothing leaves the phone. The lead then sets the default preset, the phone render scale and scaling_confirmed_on_device, warms up effects during the grid intro if compile hitches show, and adds a 30 fps battery option if 60 does not hold.
- Acceptance: GRAPHICS_BENCHMARK.md gets its first rows that name an iPhone model and iOS version. Pass line for the default preset on that phone, 20-minute soak: at most 3% of frames over 20 ms, minute-20 average fps at least 55, no frame over 100 ms after the countdown on a second run, no memory warning, owner's heat note at most 3 of 5 with the battery drop written down. If the line is missed, the default drops one preset or goes to a locked 30 fps (at most 1% of frames over 40 ms) and the run is repeated.
- Effort: 1 lead day, about 45 minutes of owner time (two runs including the soak)
- Needs the owner: Yes: run it on the phone and send the screenshots. First, say what happened when ios-release-1 was tried (PROGRESS.md records 'Denedim' with no report).

#### 3. Crash, memory and save shield; set the device floor before the first release

- Why: In fair and premium racers the low-star reviews are crashes, lost saves and unsupported devices (GRID sample: crashes 4, lost saves 2, device support 2 of 13 low reviews; Rush Rally 3: lost save 2, device support 2 of 14) [S9][S11]. With one test phone, the honest protection is a narrow declared device range plus self-healing. Apple lets an update keep or relax device requirements, never tighten them [S20], so the floor has to be right in 1.0. The iOS preset allows iOS 16.0 today, which includes iPhone 8 and X.
- How: (a) Floor at A12: capabilities/performance_a12=true in the iOS preset (iPhone XS / XR and newer [S25]) or min iOS 17.0 [S22]. (b) First launch picks the preset from the device memory the engine reports; High only where step 2 measured it. (c) Boot guard: a 'race open' flag written at race start and cleared at results or quit; if it is still set at launch, drop one preset and say so in one line. (d) On NOTIFICATION_OS_MEMORY_WARNING [S23] drop the mirror pass and one preset for the session. (e) Save: fixtures of every released save version under game/tests/fixtures/saves/ with a test that each loads with cash, cars and stars intact; tests for a truncated main file and a stray .tmp. The atomic write with .bak already exists in game/core/game_state.gd: keep it. (f) One shared tail-light atlas instead of 18 extracted copies (55 MB, PROGRESS.md session 7). (g) A menu soft-lock test.
- Acceptance: test_game_state.gd additions pass (v1 and v2 fixtures load; truncated main falls back to .bak; stray .tmp ignored). test_boot_guard.gd passes (flag set: preset one lower, once; cleared after a clean results screen). test_menu_monkey.gd: 2,000 random UI activations across menu, garage, event list and settings never leave Router busy longer than 2 s. The exported Info.plist in the CI artifact lists the A12 capability or MinimumOSVersion 17.0. Owner: play on build N, update to build N+1 through TestFlight: cash, cars and stars unchanged (a Godot forum thread reports user data wiped after an update, cause unconfirmed [S29]); force-quit mid-race: next launch shows the lowered-graphics line once and the save is intact.
- Effort: 1.5 lead days, 15 minutes of owner time
- Needs the owner: Yes: approve the device floor (store-facing, cannot be tightened later) and do the update and force-quit checks. Friends with an XR / 11 / SE-class phone as testers if any exist.

#### 4. Rating request by Apple's rules (the lever the big games live on)

- Why: Displayed averages come from the system star prompt, not from written reviews: Asphalt shows 4.77 while its latest 100 written reviews average 4.14; NFS No Limits shows 4.75 against 3.20 [S1][S3][S5][S6]. Apple shows the prompt at most three times per user in 365 days and says to ask after a successfully completed sequence, not at launch and not from a button [S15][S16]. Custom prompts are disallowed (5.6.1), forcing a rating is banned (3.2.2(x)), paid, incentivized or filtered feedback is manipulation [S17]. So: timing only, no 'do you like the game?' gate, no reward.
- How: A ReviewGate script (pure logic) plus one native call. All conditions required: results screen with a win or three stars; at least 5 finished races; second session or later; no crash guard or forced preset drop in this session; not yet asked for this app version; 2 s after the results settle. Never after a loss, never in session one, never mid-race. Native call: the MIT plugin godot-mobile-plugins/godot-inapp-review [S27] (download needs the owner's yes and a line in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md; check that it supports Godot 4.7 and which StoreKit call it makes), or a project-made iOS plugin of about 30 lines built in the existing macOS workflow. Settings gets 'Rate the game' (opens apps.apple.com/app/id<ID>?action=write-review, Apple's documented link [S15][S16]) and 'Support' (owner's e-mail or page), so an unhappy player has a channel that is not the store [S18].
- Acceptance: game/tests/test_review_gate.gd passes a table of at least 12 cases (loss: no; 4 races: no; first session: no; second request on the same version: no; win in session two after 5 races: yes, exactly once). The iOS build links the plugin and launches. On the phone the log line 'review_gate: requested' appears once under those conditions; the dialog itself does not show in TestFlight builds, as Apple documents [S16]. After release the owner sees the system dialog once on the store build.
- Effort: 1-2 lead days (the native build is the risky part)
- Needs the owner: Yes: approve the plugin download or choose the project-made plugin; give the support contact and the App Store ID; confirm the dialog after release.

#### 5. Touch controls and assists, tuned on the phone

- Why: Controls are the top gameplay complaint in both camps (NFS No Limits: 4 of 25 low reviews; Rush Rally 3: about 4 of 14) and the top praise in the well-rated small racer [S6][S9]. The current layout has never been captured or driven for evidence: fixed rectangles with text labels; by the layout fractions the two steer zones cover the bottom-left 46% x 42% of the screen, where the minimap sits, and the brake pedal the bottom-right 20% x 42%, where the speed gauge and nitro ring sit. Throttle is fully automatic with no brake help, so a first-timer reaches every corner flat out.
- How: (a) Brake assist, on by default: lift and brake when speed exceeds the track's target-speed profile (the one the AI already reads) by a margin; switch in Settings. (b) Settings: steering sensitivity in 3 steps, left-handed swap, button size, tilt with a calibrate button (tilt exists in game/vehicles/player_driver.gd without calibration UI). (c) Move the minimap and gauge or the zones so nothing overlaps; icons instead of text labels. (d) 'Control lab' inside the step 2 screen: the owner drives the same Ortakoy lap with three steering-rate sets; the card shows lap time and wall hits per set; the best becomes the default. (e) Gamepad is already mapped in input_setup.gd: list it as a feature only if it is tested on the phone.
- Acceptance: test_brake_assist.gd: with full throttle input and autopilot steering, an Ortakoy lap has 0 wall contacts with the assist on and at least 1 with it off. A test asserts no touch zone rectangle intersects the minimap or gauge rectangles (TouchControls.get_zone_rect), and the 'touch_layout' capture shows it. Owner after tuning: 5 touch laps on Ortakoy night, best lap within 15% of the autopilot lap measured in the same build, at most 1 wall hit per lap.
- Effort: 2 lead days, two 20-minute phone rounds
- Needs the owner: Yes: the tuning laps. A controller test only if the owner has one.

#### 6. First ten minutes: a win, an upgrade, a reason to come back

- Why: The prompt in step 4 only pays if the first sessions feel good; Apple's own sample waits for several successful completions [S15]. Today there are no hints at all, the first event is a 2-lap race against rivals at skill 0.80-0.76 (game/race/events.gd), the track loads behind a black fade with no loading card (game/core/router.gd), and the design document scripts a last place in mission 1 (GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT.md M01), which is wrong for a rating.
- How: Fresh profile: Play goes straight into 'Ortakoy Night' (the best real capture and the Istanbul hook): 1 lap, about 65 s, three rivals at skill 0.74 / 0.70 / 0.66, no traffic, brake assist on. Three one-line hints tied to events: first corner (hold DRIFT), nitro full (tap NITRO), wrong way (reset). Results screen guides one tap to the tyre upgrade (500 of the 5,000 starting cash). Race 2: Neon Wharf at sunset, 1 lap. Race 3: Maslak at night. A loading card with the track name instead of black. Effects warmed up during the grid intro. No rating request, no store link and no settings wall in session one.
- Acceptance: game/tests/test_first_run.gd: from a fresh profile, 2 UI activations reach the countdown; event 1 is 1 lap with rival skills at or below 0.74; a bot at skill 0.72 with brake assist finishes first or second in 9 of 10 seeds. On the phone: cold launch to driving in 30 s or less by stopwatch, no black screen longer than 1 s without the loading card. Five people who never played: at least 4 finish first or second in race 1 without help and buy the upgrade; where each one got stuck is written into a file.
- Effort: 2 lead days, about 1 hour of owner time for the five-person test
- Needs the owner: Yes: the stopwatch check and the five-person test.

#### 7. A fair ladder without a hidden grind: difficulty and economy proven by a bot

- Why: Grind and pay-to-win are what players punish in the big racers [S5][S6]. This build has a grind wall of its own: 11 events pay 27,300 cash for one win each, while the ten purchasable cars cost 260,500 and full upgrades 51,000 per car (821,500 in all; game/race/events.gd, game/vehicles/car_catalog.gd, game/core/game_state.gd). The top car alone is 18 wins of the same 3-lap final, about an hour of one race. In a free game with nothing to sell this reads as 'boring after an hour'.
- How: (a) 30 events as data on the three tracks by varying time of day, rain, laps, grid size, traffic, duel and time trial: no new art; star times taken from the autopilot. (b) Rebalance so first-time rewards carry the progression: every car reachable in 4-6 hours, repeats pay half, reputation gates follow stars. (c) Easy / Normal / Hard (AI skill -0.06 / 0 / +0.04), offered after two finishes off the podium on one event; a finish still opens the next event. (d) From the first results screen, show the next car with its price and 'you have X of Y'.
- Acceptance: game/tests/test_career_bot.gd (headless): a bot at skill 0.85 plays the ladder and buys the cheapest useful upgrade first. Asserts: at least 2 stars on every event within 3 attempts; no more than 10 replays of already-podiumed events before the ladder is done; all cars owned within 90 races. Races, repeats and the cash curve are written to evidence/test_results.json. test_events.gd: 30 events, each with TR and EN names. Owner plays the first 12 events on the phone and marks any event lost three times.
- Effort: 2-3 lead days, 30-40 minutes of owner play
- Needs the owner: No for the build. Yes for the phone play-through note.

#### 8. Night-first identity, an honest store page, the price decision

- Why: The store page sets conversion and expectations. The real night captures hold up at phone size; the day capture and the studio car do not, and no screenshot can match a licensed-Ferrari render. A page that shows what the game is brings fewer disappointed raters. The two listing drafts are out of date: one still lists police pursuit and three cars, the other ten events and old car names (docs/release/STORE_LISTING_DRAFT.md, docs/store/APP_STORE_METADATA.md). Price evidence: free-with-paywall editions rate below the paid edition of the same racer (Wreckfest: Try and Buy 3.51 vs 4.24; GRID Custom Edition 4.35 vs 4.56) [S11][S12]; two cases, indicative only.
- How: (a) Recommended: 1.0 is free with no ads, no purchases and no account. Consider a cosmetic-only supporter pack after 200 ratings at 4.7 or better; never a content gate. If money matters more than rating: paid at a low price, never free-plus-paywall. (b) The career opens with night and sunset-rain events; day events move late or leave 1.0. (c) Six screenshots from the capture harness at 2556x1179: Ortakoy night race, Maslak night, Neon Wharf night, garage paint, results with stars, event list; the first two carry 'No ads. No purchases. Offline.' and the download size. Screenshots must show the app in use [S17]. (d) One metadata file with real counts (11 cars, 30 events, 3 tracks) in TR and EN. (e) A small script fails when the listing uses a feature word that has no test or capture behind it.
- Acceptance: Six PNGs with their harness JSON beside them. 'py -3.10 tools/check_listing.py' exits 0. No listing line mentions police, multiplayer, real makes or fps. The owner's written yes on price, on the name (after the name and trademark search, RELEASE_CHECKLIST step 3, still not done) and on the screenshots.
- Effort: 1 lead day
- Needs the owner: Yes: price decision (money), name check, approval and upload in App Store Connect.

#### 9. Soft launch at no cost: TestFlight public link, then Turkey first

- Why: Big racers soft-launch to burn bugs before ratings count. The free equivalents: TestFlight external testing (up to 10,000 testers; screenshots and crash reports come back through App Store Connect; the first external build is reviewed) [S21], and a one-country release. The store rating is per country and belongs to the country of purchase [S18][S19], so a stumble in the first storefront does not mark the others. Turkey is home ground: Turkish interface, Istanbul tracks, a 4.70 to beat [S2].
- How: External group with a public link; target 30-50 real players over two weeks (the owner's circle and Turkish game communities; the owner posts). One build a week. Every crash report and written comment goes into docs/beta/FEEDBACK.md with a status. Exit: the candidate build has zero crash reports and no 'stuck / cannot continue' report over at least 100 sessions (TestFlight tester metrics in App Store Connect, or a manual count from testers if the console lacks it), and at least 80% of testers who started reached race 5. Then release in Turkey only. Widen to other countries when Turkey shows 4.7 or better from at least 30 ratings and two weeks pass without a crash trend. Testers who liked it may be asked once to rate the store build; no reward.
- Acceptance: FEEDBACK.md with every item closed or answered. Owner screenshot of an empty TestFlight crash list for the candidate build. The two exit numbers written in PROGRESS.md. RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md steps 22-24 and 37-40 ticked with evidence; BLOCKERS.md B1/B2 updated to match the upload that already happened.
- Effort: 2-3 weeks elapsed, about 3 lead days of fixes
- Needs the owner: Yes: every store action, tester invitations and community posts (messages), the release itself.

#### 10. After launch: reply, fix, measure, and keep the average

- Why: With tens of ratings each one-star moves the average by tenths. Apple notifies the reviewer when the developer replies and the reviewer can change the rating; a summary reset exists for a version that fixed a named problem, to be used sparingly [S18].
- How: Weekly: the lead reads new reviews from the public feed and drafts a reply to every review of three stars or less within 48 hours; the owner posts it. The most-named problem is fixed in a point release within a week and named in the release notes. Every release passes the full tests, the save fixtures, the 20-minute soak on the phone and the update-path check. A small script logs the weekly storefront averages for this game, Asphalt and NFS No Limits (public lookup, country=tr and us) into evidence/ratings_log.csv. Reset the summary at most once, and only after a version that fixes a named cause.
- Acceptance: ratings_log.csv has a row per week. Every review of three stars or less has a posted reply (owner screenshot). Target line: the Turkish average is above Asphalt's Turkish figure on the same day with at least 100 ratings, then the same in the US. Any week with a crash review leads to a point release within 7 days.
- Effort: 1-2 hours a week, plus point releases
- Needs the owner: Yes: posting replies (messages), submitting updates, the reset decision.

### Risks

- Volume. With no marketing budget the game may collect only tens of ratings. A 4.8 from 40 ratings beats Asphalt on the page and is fragile. I could not verify how many ratings a storefront needs before it shows an average (Apple says 'enough', no number) [S19].
- One test phone. Performance, heat and memory on every other iPhone stay unknown; the A12 floor and the boot guard reduce this, they do not remove it. Desktop numbers (340 fps on an RTX 5060 Ti) say nothing about the phone.
- The Mobile renderer at native phone resolution may not hold 60 fps with the mirror pass and post-processing. If only 30 fps holds at an acceptable look, 'lag' reviews are possible. The answer waits for step 2.
- The rating prompt cannot be seen in TestFlight [S16], so its first real proof comes after release. The plugin's Godot 4.7 support is not stated on its page [S27]. If the native call fails, ratings come only from people who write reviews, a harsher population.
- Free means zero revenue in 1.0 and conflicts with 'commercially shippable'. This is the owner's call. The paid alternative brings fewer ratings and a price-complaint class (price is named in 3 of 13 low GRID reviews and about 5 of 14 for Rush Rally 3) [S9][S11].
- Scope pull toward 'Ferrari-level cars' would eat the weeks steps 1-7 need, for a factor that barely appears in the rating evidence.
- Thin content. 30 events on 3 tracks may feel short by hour three; players of small racers ask for more content [S9][S10]. Short and fair still rates above long and grindy in the samples.
- Evidence limits. Review themes come from the 50-100 most recent written reviews per app, classified by an automated summary: counts are indicative. The free-versus-paid comparison is two pairs. Store figures were read on 2026-11-27 and move daily.
- Owner time is the bottleneck: steps 1-6 each need a phone session. If device feedback takes days, the January date moves.
- Not checked in this pass: the iOS build size, load times and audio behaviour on the phone (silent switch, the player's own music; engine defaults in [S28]), whether iOS backups include the save, iPad compatibility mode, the trademark status of the name, and how the uploaded build ios-release-1 behaves (it predates today's code). PROGRESS.md also notes builds need the iOS 27 SDK from April 2027.

## Plan: visual lens

"Beat Asphalt" means two things. Out-produce it: not winnable. Asphalt Legends is Gameloft Barcelona on its own Jet Engine with 300 licensed cars and a 3.9 GB install, and the picture the owner sent is not a race frame: it is an App Store In-App Event card (badge "New Season") with a real Ferrari, which is marketing art. Out-rate it: winnable in principle, because a rating is per storefront and comes from a game's own players (Asphalt shows 4.8 from 1.1M ratings in the US and 4.7 from 149k in Turkey, which is the owner's figure). But looks do not decide a rating: the reviews Apple shows on Asphalt's page complain about pay-to-win, ads and matchmaking, and critics praise its graphics. So the visual job is narrow: win the first 30 seconds (title, garage, first night race) and never pay for it with heat or dropped frames. I read "ultra gercekci plan" as "a plan that is realistic"; if it meant photoreal graphics, that is in the not-winnable list.

Four findings from the repository drive the plan.
1. Every capture behind the lead's gate score is the High preset, but the game starts on Medium (game/core/settings.gd:39,52). On Medium the wet-road mirror is off (game/race/race_scene.gd:629), there is no anti-aliasing of any kind, and a phone build renders 3D at scale 1.0. Nobody has captured or seen today's default night look, on the desktop or on the iPhone.
2. The reference composition (one car in a dark studio) is the cheapest gap. The dark stage already exists (ShowroomSet.build_studio: 57 draw calls / 174,224 primitives with a car) while the game shows a bright photo workshop (277 / 348,618) and a title screen with no car at all (26 draws / 630 primitives).
3. Car detail was thrown away by the pipeline, not missing. The CC BY sources carry Mesh_Hex (alpha mask, UVs), Mesh_Square, Carbon and LED materials that prepare_vehicle.py flattens to one colour. The nitro flames of all eleven cars sit at the exhaust coordinates of a car that no longer exists (game/fx/car_fx.gd:11-13), which is the white blob over the number plate in the benchmark shots.
4. The budget is full (Bosphorus night 443,576 of 450,000 primitives, Neon Wharf 481 of 500 draws), but an estimated 60,000 to 100,000 primitives per race frame are waste: the paint is drawn twice by a second pass (M_Paint is 24.6k-28.4k triangles at LOD0, 6.2k-7.9k at LOD1) and LOD1 keeps 3.9k-4.9k interior triangles behind dark glass. That pays for every addition below.

Build on what already works: night, rain, wet road, Istanbul. Parity with a licensed-brand studio is not on offer. A premium night racer that holds its frame rate on the owner's iPhone is. All performance figures here are desktop RTX 5060 Ti numbers; no iPhone number exists.

### Winnable

- Rating, not production: a 4.7+ average in the Turkish storefront is reachable for a small game, because Apple computes the summary per territory from the game's own players. Looks help only through the first impression and through not causing heat. Not verified: how many ratings Apple needs before it shows an average (Asphalt's own Mac listing shows none: 'not enough ratings').
- First seconds, title screen. Gap: a 2D shader city with no car (ui_main_menu: 26 draws / 630 primitives). Technique: MainMenu.set_backdrop() (already in the code) with the dark studio stage and the player's car on a slow orbit. Cost: about 60 draws / 175k primitives on a screen that draws nothing else. Proof: evidence/screens/ui_main_menu/iphone_2556x1179.png.
- First seconds, garage presentation and lighting contrast. Gap: bright busy workshop (brick, shutters, yellow floor lines, a stray dark oval and a magenta stripe on the floor), a frontal fill light of energy 3.0 that flattens the body, paint that reads as red plastic, chalk-white rims, large panels on both sides. The reference is the opposite: black room, one key, car owns the frame. Technique: the existing ShowroomSet.build_studio look with a neutral near-black room, one long white softbox card overhead (seen in the floor mirror and, through the room reflection probe, as a highlight along the shoulder), two unshadowed rim strips, no frontal fill, one accent in the paint's hue instead of amber/cyan/magenta, floor reflection that fades with roughness, three additive haze cards (steam_card.gdshader), PostFx vignette (race-only today). Cost: saves about 200 draws and 150k primitives against today's garage. Proof: garage/front.png next to the reference.
- Car materials. Gap (car_studio_tayfun/nose_closeup.png): flat black grille holes, no carbon weave, a white DRL rectangle without a halo, grey rims with no metal read, squiggly reflections on the lower door and bumper. Technique: new M_Mesh alpha-scissor material at LOD0 from the source's Mesh_* faces and UVs; project-made twill carbon; lamp emission above the glow threshold plus one additive halo quad; street panorama fed into the sky radiance so metal has something to reflect. Cost: +2 draws per LOD0 car and 0-16 triangles; a race frame gains about 2 draws because rivals are LOD1 beyond 6 m and the mirror uses LOD1. Proof: car_studio_<car>/nose_closeup.png, wheel_closeup.png, tail_closeup.png for all eleven cars.
- Centre of the race frame. Gap: white nitro blob with pink tips over the plate (bench_night_gameplay.png, bench_day_gameplay.png); the player car is the darkest object in city_night.png and maslak_night.png; the carbon wing reads as a flat grey plank; tail lamps are thin dim lines. Technique: flame anchors from each car's exhaust node with a blue core, a car-layer key and stronger rim light, tail-lamp emission over the glow threshold with a red road pool when braking, darker carbon with low specular. Cost: 0 primitives, +1 draw while braking. Proof: the same three captures retaken.
- Night road on the default preset. Gap: on Medium the road has no reflection at all, so the signature look is absent for most players; on High it is a mirror from kerb to kerb and reads as glass (GRAPHICS_BENCHMARK already lists 'no puddle break-up'). Technique: street panorama in the sky radiance cubemap (sky shader AT_CUBEMAP_PASS) so wet asphalt reflects the street with no mirror pass; additive streak cards under lamps and signs; on High the sharp mirror only in puddles and a blurred, dimmer reflection on damp asphalt. Cost: 0 extra passes, +4 to 8 draws for streak cards; the mirror it replaces on Medium costs 89-120 draws at peak today. Proof: tod_tracks/{city,maslak,bogaz}_night_medium.png and the High night shots.
- Light sources at night. Gap: lamps and signs are bright textures with no halo or streak; rivals at distance clip to white blobs (maslak_night.png). Technique: one MultiMesh of camera-facing additive quads per track (project-made soft-star texture, size clamped on screen, no depth-texture fade), capped rival lamp emission. Cost: +4 to 8 draws, under 2,000 triangles. Proof: ortakoy_benchmark/bench_night_street.png.
- Environment density near the camera. Gap: photo fronts are flat pictures at grazing angles and the same sign appears three times in one frame (HOTEL ARDEN, KOI NOODLE BAR, KULE KITAP). Technique: a thin 3D layer (awnings, sign brackets, wall lamps, rails, AC units from kit pieces already owned) as MultiMeshes within 40 m of the road, recessed shop glass with street_glass.gdshader, 12+ original sign names hashed per building. Cost: target +15 draws and +20k primitives, paid by the primitive saving and by merging sign surfaces (docs/perf/DRAW_BREAKDOWN.md, cut 4). Proof: a grazing-angle street_fronts shot per track and city_districts/neon_strip.png.
- Motion. Gap: the flame blob, possible spoke strobing (wheels spin physically, no blur), speed blur starts only at 110 km/h; stills cannot judge any of it. Technique: Godot Movie Maker clip as evidence, clamp of the visual wheel spin or a blur disc, flame and halo stretch with speed. Cost: 0 at runtime. Proof: a 10 s nitro clip per track under evidence/motion/.
- Aliasing on the default preset. Gap: Medium and Low have no anti-aliasing (MSAA 2x exists on High only, no FXAA or SMAA anywhere). Technique: FXAA or SMAA 1x on Medium/Low, both supported on the Mobile renderer; pick on the phone. Cost: one full-screen pass (Godot docs call FXAA the cheapest method). Proof: owner iPhone screenshot pair.
- Already strong, protect it: the night wet road with warm shop light, floodlit mosque and bridge (bench_night_gameplay.png) is the identity; the Istanbul content (Ortakoy mosque, Bosphorus bridge, Turkish shop signs, TR plates) is something no competitor has in the store where Asphalt sits at 4.7; the HUD is coherent with live data; clearcoat-and-flake paint reads deep in the dark studio (hero34.png); the capture harness, measured budgets and 335 green tests keep claims honest. Keep night and blue hour for the first race, the title and the store shots.

### Not winnable

- Out-producing Asphalt: 300 licensed cars, live seasons, multiplayer and a full studio on its own engine. No zero-budget plan closes that.
- Brand pull: the card shows a real Ferrari. Real makes and logos are forbidden by the project rules, so the 'I want that car' effect cannot be bought.
- Car model fidelity: the CC BY sources are about 129k triangles with tiny tile textures and generic surfacing (shipped at about 88k). Materials and light can make them look finished and coherent; they will not read as manufacturer-data models (lamp internals, shut lines, surfacing). Closing that needs a car artist or paid models.
- Screen-space and global lighting on this renderer: Godot's Mobile renderer has no SSR, SSAO, SSIL, SDFGI, volumetric fog or TAA, FSR is Forward+ only, and Forward+ breaks the scene (BLOCKERS B12). So no true reflections of moving cars in paint or on the road outside the planar mirror, no real ambient occlusion, no light shafts. Authored fakes only.
- Baked global illumination at this price: LightmapGI is supported on Mobile but needs editor-baked UV2 meshes, and the tracks are assembled in code at load. It would mean rebuilding the world pipeline; not in this plan.
- Noon daylight at Asphalt's level: without ambient occlusion and bounce light the day look stays a generation behind (bench_day_gameplay.png). The plan retires noon instead of fixing it.
- In-engine parity with the reference picture: it is offline-grade key art. Its composition can be matched in the garage; its class of image can only be matched by an offline Blender render of our own car for store art, and store screenshots must still show the app in use (App Review guideline 2.3.3).
- Per-object motion blur, animated crowds and city life at Asphalt's density.
- Any iPhone claim today: there is no frame time, heat or memory figure and not even a look at the default preset on a device. Until the owner runs step 1, 'looks good on iPhone' cannot be said.

#### 4. Street light through the sky radiance: wet road on Medium, metal that reflects, primitives back

- Why: One engine feature fixes three things. The paint mirrors the street through a second pass (car_reflection.gdshader as next_pass), which redraws 24.6k-28.4k triangles per LOD0 car and 6.2k-7.9k per LOD1 car; metal parts have nothing to reflect but the sky, so rims look like chalk; and the Medium road has no reflection. Bosphorus is at 443,576 of 450,000 primitives, so nothing else in this plan fits until this is done.
- How: In night_sky.gdshader and day_sky.gdshader output the track's street panorama (pre-blurred, bright parts lifted) when AT_CUBEMAP_PASS is true, and raise the sky radiance size from 64 to 256. Every PBR surface then reflects the street through the standard path: clearcoat paint, rims, chrome, glass and wet asphalt. Delete the next_pass in CarMaterials.set_reflection. Fallback if the Mobile radiance looks wrong: a single-pass paint shader built from the StandardMaterial3D code with the panorama added to EMISSION. Pipeline: LOD1 drops M_Interior for a 2-triangle dark cabin card with glass alpha 0.92; rebuild with rebuild_vehicles.py. Add FXAA or SMAA 1x to Medium and Low (final choice from step 1).
- Acceptance: draw_breakdown JSON before and after committed. bench_night_gameplay.json primitives at 390,000 or fewer (443,576 today) and the Neon Wharf night peak at 470 draws or fewer (481 today), with before/after PNG sheets judged equal or better by the lead and one independent reviewer. tod_tracks --preset=medium night shots show coloured light on the road: mean luma of the road region at least 1.5 times the Medium baseline recorded in step 1, within 350 draws. car_studio_*/lod1.png shows no see-through cabin. test_car_materials, test_car_licensed and the garage reflection test adapted and green.
- Effort: 1.5 days
- Needs the owner: No for the work; the owner's next device screenshot (step 1 loop) confirms the Medium look on the phone.

#### 5. Night road and light sources: puddle break-up, streak cards, lamp halos

## Plan: release lens

Out-producing Asphalt is not winnable. Out-rating it in the Turkish store is winnable, but only on the phone, and the phone build is where this project is weakest today. Facts: Asphalt Legends shows 4.7 from 149k ratings in the TR store (4.8 from 1.1M in the US), is 3.9 GB, free with purchases and tracking labels; its visible critical reviews are about ads, pay-to-win and matchmaking, not graphics. A summary rating is per territory and comes from a game's own players, so a small offline racer can show 4.7 or more (GRID Autosport 4.6 from 2.9K ratings, Rush Rally 3 4.8 from 3K) if nothing fails in the hand; GRID's own low reviews are heat, frame dips and weak touch controls. Read from this repository today (code and captures, no device): a race cannot be paused, restarted, quit or reset by touch; the BRAKE touch zone lies over the nitro gauge and most of the speedometer; a phone renders 3D at native resolution with dynamic resolution locked off; 247 of 358 textures are imported lossless and sit uncompressed in GPU memory; Low and Medium, the presets phones will run, were last measured on 2026-11-26, four sessions of content ago; there is no iPhone frame, heat or memory number, and the TestFlight build is 7 commits behind today's cars. The Ferrari card is licensed key art of a real car: it cannot be reached in gameplay and may not be used as a screenshot. So the plan is: fix the phone blockers, make the build measure itself, set gates from the owner's device data, test privately on TestFlight, release in Turkey first, widen only while the average holds. Steps 1 to 3 need no owner input to start. Expect at least three owner device rounds before a public build.

### Winnable

- Displayed average of 4.7 or more in the Turkish App Store with a small base (tens to hundreds of ratings). Apple: the summary rating is specific to each territory and can be reset with a new version. The rating count is not part of this.
- Download and install size. Current content is about 0.3 to 0.4 GB before the engine binary (378 MB in this morning's Windows pack) against 3.9 GB Asphalt Legends, 3.8 GB NFS No Limits, 1.5 GB CarX Street, 4 GB GRID Autosport. Target: 200 MB download or less.
- Fairness and privacy: no ads, no purchases, no energy, no login, offline, 'Data Not Collected'. Asphalt's page lists seven data types used for tracking; the critical reviews on the Asphalt and NFS No Limits pages are about ads, paywalled cars, tickets, fuel and blueprints.
- Heat, battery and steady frame time against heavy 3D racers. The scene budget is small (500 draw calls, 450k primitives). Winnable only after the device gates of step 4 are met; unproven today.
- Launch and load time: small pack, compressed textures, a loading screen.
- Touch controls that work without a controller. GRID Autosport's reviews call its touch controls weak; ours can be tuned on real thumbs before anyone rates them.
- Home market: Turkish interface, Istanbul circuits, Turkey-first release.
- Care: a reply to every low review within 48 hours and a fix build each week. Apple notifies the reviewer of a reply and lets them change the rating.
- A 4+ age rating is plausible (CarX Street and GRID Autosport are 4+; Apple defines realistic violence as harm involving humans) against Asphalt's 13+. The owner answers the questionnaire.

### Not winnable

- Old phones. Godot 4.7 names A12 as the minimum and A14 as the recommended chip for its Metal path; Asphalt runs from iOS 15. A11 phones must be excluded and A12 to A13 phones may end on Low at 30 fps.

#### 9. Rating mechanics: Apple's prompt for happy players, fast answers for unhappy ones

A developer reply notifies the reviewer, who can change the rating. The summary rating can be reset with a new version; Apple says to use that sparingly.
- How: LEAD ALONE: call the system prompt through a native plugin. Candidate: godot-inapp-review (MIT; its asset library entry lists Godot 4.6; support for 4.7.2 and its behaviour on TestFlight are unverified). Trigger: the third podium finish, at least 20 minutes of total play, two seconds after the results settle, never after a loss, once per app version. Fallback with no native code: the Rate the game row in Settings opens https://apps.apple.com/app/id<APPID>?action=write-review, the manual path Apple documents. Reply templates in TR and EN. OWNER ONLY: approve the plugin download and its licence record; post the replies. Rule: every review of three stars or less is answered within 48 hours, and the next build's What's New names the fix.
- Acceptance: A unit test of the trigger rule (counts, once per version, never after a loss). - Effort: Lead: 0.5 to 1 session. Owner: one approval, then about 10 minutes a day after launch.

### Risks

- Owner round-trips are the critical path. If a device run comes back without data, as the first try did, every later step stalls. The Device Test screen exists to make a run three taps and one file.
- The owner's phone is a recent 6.1-inch model (the uploaded screenshot is 1179x2556; the exact model is unconfirmed) and cannot stand in for A12 to A15 phones. Without borrowed phones in the beta, classes A and B ship untested.
- Device requirements cannot be tightened after release (Apple). A wrong floor in 1.0 is a permanent source of one-star reviews. The plist key behind Godot's performance_a12 option was not read in an exported project: check it in the CI log.
- Name: Midnight Syndicate is an existing music act with game-related products; trademark status is unverified. A late rename redoes the icon, splash, store text and screenshots.
- ETC2 compression may show blocks on photo facades and night glow; ASTC costs size. On the desktop the lead can only judge the S3TC twin.
- First launch on Metal may spend many seconds compiling shaders; the Shader Baker is not available in the headless CI export. Unmeasured.
- The review-prompt plugin is third-party code listed for Godot 4.6, not checked against 4.7.2 or in an Xcode link step. If it fails only the manual link remains and fewer happy players rate.
- The numeric gates (18 ms, 10 % battery, load seconds, memory targets) are the lead's proposals. Apple publishes no per-app memory limit, so memory is gated by 'no kill in N runs', not by megabytes.
- The Medium preset anomaly on the desktop (25 ms average, 60 ms p95, Bosphorus sunset, 2026-11-26 15:30) may be GPU contention or a real slow path. Phones default to Medium.
- Thin content (11 events, 3 tracks) draws 'too short' reviews, more so with a price. Outside this lens, but it caps the rating once the phone problems are gone.
- Free macOS CI depends on the repository staying public (BLOCKERS B8 workaround). Going private stops iOS builds or costs money. Public also means the code and assets can be copied.
- PROGRESS.md records an upload warning: builds need the iOS 27 SDK from April 2027, and macos-latest will change under the workflow. Pin Xcode and re-check in March 2027.
- Generated facade textures, and a mosque and a flag as race scenery, can draw 'AI art' or cultural complaints in Turkish reviews. Not assessed here.
- Not checked in this planning run: nothing was run on a device or in Godot; the size of the iOS binary (read App File Sizes); whether the Capture autoload error appears on the phone; the overlap of touch zones and gauges is computed from constants, not seen on a screen; the default 'Ask If Over 200 MB' cellular setting comes from secondary sources; RAM sizes of iPhones are not published by Apple; the Rush Rally 3 figures come from a search snippet of its store page.
