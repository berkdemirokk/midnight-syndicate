## 4. Description

### English

Midnight Syndicate is an arcade racer set in a rain-soaked city after dark. Take the wheel, read the wet
asphalt, and push your car through corners with drift and nitro.

Features (each line is tagged with its milestone; none is built yet):
- Arcade driving with drift and a nitro boost that drains and refills. [VS]
- A closed city circuit with checkpoints, laps and a live race position. [VS]
- Three cars with different handling, shown in the garage. [VS]
- Rival drivers who race the line and overtake you. [VS]
- Night lighting with neon, wet road reflections and rain. [VS, look depends on device tests]
- Police pursuit with cars that chase you through the city. [LATER]
- Career progression across several stages. [LATER]

The game works offline. It needs no account and collects no personal data. [VS: offline is a design rule;
verify before publishing]
