# Visual target (owner reference, 2026-11-28)

The owner supplied a reference board of three frames from commercial mobile/console arcade racers (a day drift
frame on a blossom-lined road, a wet neon night street, a neon garage showroom). The board contains other companies'
screenshots and car brands, so it is NOT stored in this public repository; it lives only in the lead's session
scratchpad. This file records what the frames show, so every package aims at the same target. Nothing from those games
may be copied: only the qualities below.

## Day drift frame
- Hero car: dense, realistic body with crisp panel lines, saturated glossy paint with a sharp sky/scene reflection and
  a hot specular on the roof and rear arch; detailed tail lamps; exhaust flame.
- Foliage: big blossom trees (pink canopy) overhanging the road, falling petals, a stone wall and planting on the
  verge. Foliage carries the frame: it is volumetric, back-lit and colourful, never a flat billboard.
- Drift: thick tyre smoke trailing the rear wheels, skid marks on the road.
- Road: worn asphalt with visible texture and wet patches catching the sky.
- HUD: huge italic speed number, a red timer badge, a slanted drift banner with a coloured accent, minimal clutter.

## Night street frame
- Wet road mirroring neon and street lights in long streaks, yellow centre lines, palms and street trees as dark
  silhouettes, bright neon accents in the distance, car with glowing tail lamps and wheel-arch shadow.
- Strong depth: clear foreground car, mid-ground lights, far skyline glow; colour is teal/blue with warm accents.

## Garage frame
- Several cars side by side under a lit canopy, neon strips, a polished floor reflecting the cars, a big branded wall
  (our own brand only: Midnight Syndicate / Noctra), cars at a low three-quarter angle.

## What this means for our packages (gap list)
1. Cars: model density and paint quality are the biggest gap (carpaint, fleet, carmerge packages).
2. Foliage: replace blurry billboard trees with volumetric blossom/street trees (impostors from real CC0 tree models).
3. Drift smoke and particles: thicker, lit smoke on drifts; petals/leaves on the Ortakoy day route.
4. Night: wet-road streaks and neon depth (already strong on Maslak/Wharf; keep).
5. HUD: italic speed, timer badge, drift banner (hud package).
6. Garage: multi-car showroom with reflective floor and neon (garage package).
