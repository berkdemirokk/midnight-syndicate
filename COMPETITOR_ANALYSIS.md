## 1. Reference profiles

### Need for Speed No Limits (NFSNL)
- Launched Sep 30, 2015, published by EA [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Need_for_Speed:_No_Limits . Developer unresolved: infobox says Firemonkeys, lead says Codemasters [S] same URL.
- Free-to-play. Metacritic 67 from 8 reviews [S] same URL. Trusted Reviews (3/5) faulted microtransactions needed to unlock all cars [S] same URL.
- Controls: tap to steer; acceleration and braking automated; swipe down to drift, swipe up for nitro [S] https://www.pocketgamer.com/need-for-speed-no-limits/review/
- Races "fly by in less than a minute" [S] https://www.pocketgamer.com/need-for-speed-no-limits/review/ . Police jump over ramps instead of stopping [S] same URL.
- Modes: campaign, Car Series, ghost-based Rival Races, time-limited events that loan a car [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Need_for_Speed:_No_Limits . Interceptor mode (Jan 23, 2023) lets the player join police [S] same URL.

### Need for Speed Underground 2 (NFSU2, 2004)
- EA Black Box, Nov 2004. Open-world Bayview; races unlocked by driving to them [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Need_for_Speed:_Underground_2
- Modes: circuit (up to 4 cars, 2-5 laps), sprint, drag (manual shift), drift (nitrous banned), Street X (nitrous off), URL tournaments (up to 6 cars), outruns, photographer events [S] same URL.
- Five stages with a world map; 11 unique sponsor races per stage, player runs 3 (corrected in verification) [S] same URL.
- Visual parts are cosmetic; only spoilers and hoods affect downforce. Dyno shows power and torque graphs [S] same URL.
- Nitrous refilled by style points from stunts [S] same URL.
- Critics: no fast travel to races, product placement, hip-hop slang, comic cutscenes, no police. Metacritic DS 65, Xbox 83 [S] same URL.
- Sales 8.4 million by end 2004 per EA [S] same URL.
- Underground helped mainstream the tuner scene after The Fast and the Furious [S] https://www.thedrive.com/news/need-for-speed-underground-changed-racing-games-20-years-ago.md

### Asphalt Legends (Asphalt 9)
- Worldwide release July 25, 2018. 2019 Apple Design Award, first racing game to win one [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asphalt_9:_Legends
- Renamed Asphalt Legends Unite Jul 17, 2024; "Unite" dropped Jul 24, 2025 [S] same URL.
- Three drive modes: tap-to-steer, tilt-to-steer, TouchDrive [S] same URL. TouchDrive takes acceleration and steering; swipes choose routes [S] https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/076788/r/ . "Stunts" as a swipe type: not in that article (gap).
- Cars in classes D to S; 48 at launch, 52 at worldwide release, 300 after Legacy of Speed. Blueprints unlock cars [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asphalt_9:_Legends
- Modes: Career, Multiplayer, Daily Events, Gauntlet, Seasonal [S] same URL.
- Nitro: drift, stunts and ramps fill it [E] https://afkgaming.com/gaming/general/asphalt-9-legends-guide-the-most-efficient-ways-to-use-nitro (snippet). Perfect nitro is a timed hit at the blue mark; Shockwave from a full bar [E] https://racinggames.gg/article/how-to-get-a-perfect-nitro-in-asphalt-9-legends (snippet). Nitro Shockwave was previously Nitro Pulse [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asphalt_9:_Legends
- P-CCOMP esports is manual controls without TouchDrive [S] same Wikipedia URL.

### CSR Racing 2 (CSR2, 2016 data)
- Car look: PBR in Unity, car paint simulation, static image-based lighting, fallbacks for weak GPUs (GDC 2016 abstract only) [S] https://gdcvault.com/play/1022998/Delivering-Console-Car-Visuals-on
- Cars were the biggest content cost: full interiors and real paint options [S] https://www.pocketgamer.biz/the-making-of-csr-racing-2/
- Start and shift: timed gauge with orange and green zones; nitro not used at the start [S] https://www.pocketgamer.com/csr-racing-2/how-to-accelerate-and-change-gears-optimally-in-csr-racing-2
- Tuning: nitro balance, final drive, tire pressure, dyno, wheelspin icon [S] https://www.pocketgamer.com/csr-racing-2/how-to-tune-your-car-in-csr-racing-2-tires-nitro-and-final-drive
- 2016 monetization: Cash, Gold ($2.99 to $99.99), gacha Keys (bronze $0.99, silver $2.99, gold $9.99); some cars up to $30; fuel pips refilled by Gold or a rewarded ad [S] https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-does-csr-racing-2-monetise/
- Zynga apologised after a random per-player price test [E] https://www.pocketgamer.biz/zynga-csr2-random-prices/ (snippet, date unconfirmed)

### CarX Street
- Free with IAP $0.99 to $14.99; Premium $1.99 for 7 days; Road Warrior pack $7.99. 1.5 GB, age 4+, iOS 15+, 3 GB RAM minimum, stable connection needed [S] https://apps.apple.com/app/id1458863319
- Open-world Sunset City with club, drift, sprint and free-drift races, delivery quests and leaderboard mode [S] https://store.steampowered.com/app/1114150/CarX_Street/
- Developer claims "CarX Technology" physics and per-mode tuning; claim not verified [S claim] same Steam URL. Steam: "Mostly Positive", 9,785 English reviews, 70% positive [S] same URL.
- Traffic reacts to hits; rival drivers ram near the finish [S] https://www.thexboxhub.com/carx-street-review/ . Tilt is the best default; movable buttons; Bluetooth controllers did not work at the 2022 iOS launch [S] https://traxion.gg/carx-street-review-ios-leads-the-way-for-carxs-move-to-open-world/
- Club progression: join a club, upgrade, beat members to the boss; underpowered by club two [S] https://traxion.gg/carx-street-review-ios-leads-the-way-for-carxs-move-to-open-world/
- Heat and battery drain on iPhone; frame hitches on an iPhone 13 mini (2022 review) [S] same Traxion URL.

## 3. Top 12 user complaints (ranked by spread across titles and source strength)

1. Energy and blueprint grind walls [Asphalt]. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asphalt_9:_Legends [S]. Design answer [E]: no energy meter; unlocks come from play with a visible requirement, never a timer. Offline makes a server-side meter pointless anyway.
2. Paid unlocks in a free-to-play game [NFSNL]. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Need_for_Speed:_No_Limits [S] (Trusted Reviews 3/5). Design answer [E]: every car earned in play; one premium purchase or a fair IAP; no car behind a paywall.
3. Underpowered after club two; must grind or spend [CarX]. Source: https://traxion.gg/carx-street-review-ios-leads-the-way-for-carxs-move-to-open-world/ [S]. Design answer [E]: show the required car level before each race; grind curve reaches each boss fairly.
4. Heat, battery drain and frame hitches on iPhone [CarX]. Source: https://traxion.gg/carx-street-review-ios-leads-the-way-for-carxs-move-to-open-world/ [S]; store-review repeats [E] https://apps.apple.com/app/id1458863319 . Design answer [E]: frame cap and thermal-aware quality drop; profile on a real iPhone.
5. Tilt steering is awkward and causes wall crashes [Asphalt, critic]. Source: https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/076788/r/ [S]. Design answer [E]: assist on by default; tilt only with heavy smoothing and a 3-5 degree dead zone, otherwise off.
6. Drift countersteer snaps the car straight [CarX]. Source: https://www.thexboxhub.com/carx-street-review/ [S] (reviewer: "a little weird"). Design answer [E]: tune countersteer and yaw recovery so the player can catch a slide.
7. Races too short; they "fly by in less than a minute" [NFSNL]. Sources: https://www.pocketgamer.com/need-for-speed-no-limits/review/ [S]; short races in reception https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Need_for_Speed:_No_Limits [S]. Design answer [E]: 60-120 s race target with a visible reward; add a longer format.
8. Police jump ramps; pursuit has no real stakes [NFSNL]. Source: https://www.pocketgamer.com/need-for-speed-no-limits/review/ [S]. Design answer [E]: terrain-aware police (ramps, barriers) and a clear pursuit end state.
9. Random per-player prices [CSR2]. Source: https://www.pocketgamer.biz/zynga-csr2-random-prices/ [E, snippet]. Design answer [E]: one visible price per item, identical for every player.
10. Gacha keys with unclear odds [CSR2]. Sources: https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-does-csr-racing-2-monetise/ [S] (key tiers and prices); https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ [S] (paid random items must disclose odds). Design answer [E]: no paid random items; if any ever exist, disclose odds.
11. Crashes, login failures and purchases not delivered [CarX]. Source: https://www.sikayetvar.com/en/carx-street-us [S; rough count by reader: crashes about 5, login about 4, purchases about 3 of 19]. Design answer [E]: offline, no login; keep IAP minimal; test purchase restore on device before launch.
12. No fast travel to races; long trips between events [NFSU2]. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Need_for_Speed:_Underground_2 [S]. Design answer [E]: map fast travel and skip-to-race from day one.

## 4. Positioning: the gap to own
